# The Witcher 3 quests: research notes

## Scope and method

These are notes taken to gather raw material, not a design. They cover about forty quests from The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt (2015) and its expansions Hearts of Stone (2015) and Blood and Wine (2016): main quests, secondary quests, witcher contracts, small quests and the main line of each expansion. After them comes a section on what CD Projekt Red's developers and some critics have said about how the studio builds quests. For each quest the notes say who gives it and what they ask, how it plays out (investigation, the turn, the choices), what follows (straight away and later), any notable device, and the sources. **How this was gathered:** the session's network policy blocked every direct page fetch (witcher.fandom.com, wiki.gg, Fextralife, Wikipedia, the guide sites and the press sites all failed DNS or were refused by the egress proxy). So every fact here comes from web-search queries, whose engine read the pages listed under each entry and summarised them. The URLs are the pages the search engine drew on; none was opened directly. Where those summaries disagreed, the entry says so. Where a summary corrected a quest name from the brief (for example, there is no contract called "The Beast of Honorfall"; the Morkvarg contract is "In Wolf's Clothing"), the entry uses the in-game name. A few details are marked _(unverified)_: they come from general knowledge of the game, no search result confirmed them, and they should be checked before anyone relies on them.

## Main quests

### Family Matters (the Bloody Baron)

- **Where and kind:** Act I, Velen (Crow's Perch). Main quest, several hours long. Designed by Paweł Sasko, written by Karolina Stachyra.
- **Premise:** Phillip Strenger, the "Bloody Baron", offers what he knows about Ciri if Geralt finds his missing wife Anna and daughter Tamara. He says they were taken.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt follows Ciri's trail through the keep and the woods with witcher senses, then turns to the Baron's family. In the ruined family quarters the clues show that Anna and Tamara _fled_ from a violent, drunken husband and father. Anna was pregnant, and she went to the Crones of Crookback Bog, who ended the pregnancy in return for her service. The buried foetus has become a botchling. **Choice:** kill the botchling, or have the Baron carry it back to the keep and perform a naming ritual so it becomes a lubberkin (a guardian spirit). On that route Geralt has to keep it calm with Axii; if he is slow, it turns hostile and the quest goes down the kill route. The lubberkin leads to a smokehouse, a horse's corpse and a fisherman's hut. The kill route goes to the Pellar for a ritual (light braziers, defend it). Both routes meet at the fisherman's hut. Tamara is found in Oxenfurt with the witch hunters and won't come home.
- **Consequences:** The botchling choice changes how this quest plays, not much where the story goes. The Baron's ending is decided later by the Whispering Hillock (see below) and plays out in Return to Crookback Bog.
- **Devices:** The client is also the villain, and becomes a sympathetic one. The "kidnapping" is really a flight from abuse, which the clues reveal. Sasko says he built the Baron as Velen made flesh and as Geralt's mirror (both fathers who lost their families). A German interview says the team toned him down from an early, more violent version so he would stay sympathetic. The quest started as a one-sentence pitch. PC Gamer and Wikipedia call it either a story of redemption or a bleak tragedy, depending on the path. It won the 2015 Golden Joystick for Best Gaming Moment.

### Hunting a Witch

- **Where and kind:** Act I, Velen (Midcopse). Main quest, suggested level 5. It starts automatically after "The Nilfgaardian Connection".
- **Premise:** Hendrik's notes say Ciri quarrelled with a witch near Midcopse.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt gathers rumour in the village. A lazy husband talks for a 50-crown bribe or under Axii, and gossiping women talk about the witch only if they think no one is listening. The trail runs past a pond and a boulder to a cart guarded by nekkers, then to a hut where villagers ask the "witch" for favours. She helps them grudgingly and vanishes. Inspecting a skull by a pentagram opens a portal. The witch turns out to be the sorceress Keira Metz in disguise, and at the end she offers to take Geralt to the ruins.
- **Devices:** Eavesdropping depends on staying unseen. The "witch" of the rumours is a hiding sorceress.
- **Sources:** https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/huntingawitch.php ; https://gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/velen/hunting-a-witch ; https://ggrecon.com/guides/the-witcher-3-wandering-in-the-dark

### Wandering in the Dark

- **Where and kind:** Act I, Velen. Main quest, suggested level 6.
- **Premise:** Keira takes Geralt into elven ruins where an elven mage gave Ciri shelter.
- **How it unfolds:** It begins as a hunt for clues about Ciri, and after the first cutscene it becomes a chase of the Wild Hunt. The boss is Nithral, the first Hunt warrior Geralt fights. In the mage's study, witcher senses find a false wall and the Eye of Nehaleni, an item that reveals illusions. A projection shows the mage warning Ciri about the "Witches of Crookback Bog", which hands the trail on.
- **Consequences:** It leads to Ladies of the Wood whether or not you help Keira. The Eye of Nehaleni is reused later (for example in the Kaer Morhen quest "Greenhouse Effect").
- **Devices:** A recorded vision that hands on the next lead. An item earned in one quest solves another.
- **Sources:** https://ggrecon.com/guides/the-witcher-3-wandering-in-the-dark ; https://vulkk.com/2022/12/14/how-to-defeat-nithral-in-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/ ; https://segmentnext.com/2015/06/02/witcher-3-kaer-morhen-secondary-quests-walkthrough

### Ladies of the Wood

- **Where and kind:** Act I, Velen (Crookback Bog). Main quest, suggested level 6.
- **Premise:** Find out from the Crones what they know of Ciri.
- **How it unfolds:** Johnny, a godling, has lost his voice; it is sealed in a bottle in a harpies' nest up a cliff. Once Geralt gets it back, Johnny says Ciri was wounded and headed for the orphans' village. The orphans help only if Geralt helps them first (hide-and-seek with four children, or bring sweets). They distract Gran, the matron, and one boy says where Johnny's burrow is. When Gran touches the tapestry, the Crones speak through her. They will talk about Ciri if Geralt deals with a creature near Downwarren, and they give him a dagger so the ealdorman knows who sent him. Afterwards the ealdorman takes the dagger and cuts off his own ear, the price the Crones expect.
- **Consequences:** It opens The Whispering Hillock, which decides the fates of the Baron's family, Downwarren and the orphans.
- **Devices:** A chain of favours (godling, then orphans, then Crones). The Crones speak through a puppet. Their price for help shows only after the deal is done.
- **Sources:** https://gamesradar.com/witcher-3-ladies-of-the-wood ; https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/ladiesofthewood.php ; https://gamerguides.com/witcher3/guide/walkthrough/velen/ladies-of-the-wood-the-whispering-hillock ; https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Ladies+of+the+Wood

### The Whispering Hillock

- **Where and kind:** Act I, Velen (Downwarren). Formally a side quest, though most players meet it inside Ladies of the Wood. It can also be started on its own.
- **Premise:** The ealdorman says an evil power behind the killings lives in the Whispering Hillock, and the Crones want it gone.
- **How it unfolds:** The "evil" is the spirit of the druidess who protected the land until the Crones moved in and murdered her. She is bound in a tree. There are three ways to end it. **Free her:** gather her bones, a raven's feather and a wild black horse for a ritual. **Trick her:** go through the ritual but don't stab the heart, which kills her without a fight. **Fight her:** attack the roots that guard her while endrega workers harass you. Guide rewards: 250 XP and hunting boots and gauntlets.
- **Consequences (delayed):** **Freed**, the spirit takes revenge on Downwarren (one source says it possesses the villagers, leaving the ealdorman alone). The orphans are saved, but Anna is turned into a water hag; lifting that curse kills her, and the Baron hangs himself. **Killed or tricked**, the Crones take the orphans (several guides say they eat them), and Anna is found alive but mad, and the Baron takes her away to seek a cure. Guides disagree about details of the trick and kill routes. A Steam thread reports an edge case: freeing the spirit before speaking to the ealdorman changes Anna's fate, and CDPR reportedly said this is intended.
- **Devices:** No right answer: each choice trades one group's lives for another's. The thing you were sent to kill is a victim. The cost lands many hours later, in another quest.
- **Sources:** https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/the-whispering-hillock ; https://www.thegamer.com/witcher-3-whispering-hillock-quest-guide/ ; https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ghost_in_the_Tree ; https://allthings.how/?p=231331 ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-bloody-baron-ending-questline ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/292030/discussions/0/490125103635625688

### Return to Crookback Bog

- **Where and kind:** Act I, Velen. Secondary quest that closes the Baron's story.
- **Premise:** At the end of Family Matters, Geralt tells the Baron what became of Anna. The Baron wants to go to the bog and rescue her from the Crones.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt and the Baron go back to the bog. How it looks depends on the Hillock: if the spirit was freed, Downwarren is burning and most of its people are dead. On the freed route, Anna is a water hag. To lift the curse Geralt must choose a doll (one guide says the violet hollyhock bloom is the right one), and lifting it kills her. On the killed route, Anna is found alive but mad, the quest ends once the fiend is beaten, and the Baron takes her away for treatment.
- **Consequences:** Freed route: Anna dies and the Baron hangs himself (Geralt finds him at the keep). Killed route: the Baron lives and leaves with Anna to seek a cure, and the orphans are gone. Nothing done in this quest undoes the earlier choice.
- **Devices:** The ending is a delayed reveal of a choice made in another quest. A choice made inside this quest (the doll) can't change the outcome already set.
- **Sources:** https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/return-to-crookback-bog ; https://earth.gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/velen/return-to-crookback-bog ; https://gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/returntocrookbackbog.php ; https://allthings.how/?p=231331

### Bald Mountain

- **Where and kind:** Act III, Velen. Main quest, climax of the Crones' thread.
- **Premise:** Geralt and Ciri go after Imlerith, a general of the Wild Hunt, at the Crones' sabbath.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt beats Imlerith in a duel while Ciri kills two of the three Crones. The third, Weavess, escapes with Vesemir's medallion.
- **Consequences:** Gran (Anna) and the Crones' thread come to a head here. The Bloody Baron side of the story follows the Hillock outcome described above.
- **Devices:** A villain left alive in Act I is paid off in Act III. One Crone escapes, leaving a thread open.
- **Sources:** https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Bald+Mountain+(Quest) ; https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/bald-mountain/ ; https://breezewiki.discard.no/witcher/wiki/Crones ; https://www.shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale/?p=43390

### The Beast of White Orchard

- **Where and kind:** Prologue, White Orchard. Main quest, written as a monster hunt.
- **Premise:** The Nilfgaardian commander will say where Yennefer went only if Geralt brings him the griffin's head.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt follows the Nilfgaardian scouts' tracks with witcher senses, across a broken bridge to the griffin's nest, and learns _why_ the griffin attacks: the scouts killed its brooding mate, and griffins pair for life. The herbalist Tomira sends him to dive in a river for glowing buckthorn as bait. Vesemir teaches the Thunderbolt potion, picks the ambush site and hands Geralt a crossbow. The griffin is shot down, flees at half health and dies by a windmill.
- **Consequences:** The commander gives Yennefer's whereabouts. The reward is crowns or extra XP, plus a griffin trophy.
- **Devices:** The monster has a reason, found by investigation, but the job still gets done. It is the template for later contracts (investigate, read the bestiary, prepare, set a trap, fight).
- **Sources:** https://witcher.gamepedia.com/The_Beast_of_White_Orchard ; https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/The+Beast+of+White+Orchard ; https://eip.gg/the-witcher-3/guides/the-beast-of-white-orchard/

### Get Junior

- **Where and kind:** Act I, Novigrad. Main quest, suggested level 12. It follows "Broken Flowers".
- **Premise:** Ciri and Dandelion made a deal with Whoreson Junior, one of Novigrad's "Big Four" crime bosses. Geralt has to find him and ask where Ciri went.
- **How it unfolds:** Dijkstra's lead gives three places to search: Junior's hideout, his casino and his arena. A meeting in a bathhouse with Sigi Reuven (Dijkstra) is broken up by Junior's men. The optional "Gangs of Novigrad" runs alongside and fails if left undone when Get Junior moves on. The climax is Junior cornered and a choice: kill him or spare him. _(The grisly staging of the final confrontation is from general knowledge, unverified.)_
- **Consequences:** Killing or sparing Junior changes how part of the later main quest "Payback" plays.
- **Devices:** Three leads open at once. A side quest that expires when the main quest moves on. A mercy choice that pays off in a much later quest.
- **Sources:** https://witcher-games.fandom.com/wiki/Get_Junior ; https://gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/novigrad/get-junior ; https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/the-gangs-of-novigrad/ ; https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quests/payback/

### The King is Dead – Long Live the King, and the Skellige succession

- **Where and kind:** Act I–II, Skellige. A main quest followed by a chain of secondary quests: Possession, The Lord of Undvik, King's Gambit, Coronation.
- **Premise:** King Bran's wake opens a succession. Crach an Craite asks Geralt to help his children, Cerys and Hjalmar, each of whom wants to win a claim.
- **How it unfolds:** The main quest ends at the wake, then two claim quests open. **Possession (Cerys):** Jarl Udalryk of Spikeroog is haunted. Clues lead to his family's ruined house and his dead brother Aki. The haunting is a hym, a spectre that feeds on guilt. Cerys's way is a trick: she tosses Udalryk's baby to Geralt and orders him to throw it into the oven, and Hjort, hidden behind the oven, catches it. The "witcher's way" is to fight the hym. **The Lord of Undvik (Hjalmar):** hunt the ice giant of Undvik; saving Folan and Vigi along the way sends them to fight later. **King's Gambit:** a massacre at a feast at Kaer Trolde. Investigating finds Arnvald, who confesses and hands over an unsigned letter telling him to poison the mead. The jarls reject that evidence, but Birna's son Svanrige turns on her and she confesses. She is sentenced to death and the an Craite name is cleared.
- **Consequences:** Who rules depends on which sibling Geralt helped and backs in King's Gambit. Guides say that if Geralt stays out and leaves the investigation to clan an Craite, Svanrige becomes king. A guide warns that entering the feast locks the chain, so prerequisites (such as "The Cave of Dreams") must be done first.
- **Devices:** An election decided by the player's earlier help to each candidate. A case where proof isn't enough, and a relative's turn is what convicts. The candidates' different styles (cunning or force) shown through how each solves their problem.
- **Sources:** https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/The+King+is+Dead+-+Long+Live+the+King ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-skellige-succession-questline ; https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/possession ; https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/possession.php ; https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/king-s-gambit ; https://witcher.gamepedia.com/King%27s_Gambit ; https://gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/skellige/kings-gambit

### Through Time and Space

- **Where and kind:** Act III. Main quest.
- **Premise:** After Imlerith falls, Avallac'h reveals a plan: Ge'els, one of Eredin's two remaining generals, stayed loyal to King Auberon, whom Eredin poisoned, so he might turn.
- **How it unfolds:** It begins at a portal in the cellar of the haunted house from "Novigrad Dreaming". Geralt crosses four worlds: a desert, a poisoned valley (where he and Avallac'h split up), drowned elven ruins and an icy plain with a lighthouse. Then a last portal to Tir ná Lía, where Geralt talks to Ge'els. Caranthir is said to stay loyal to the Hunt.
- **Devices:** A journey told through set-piece worlds. Turning an enemy lieutenant through a grudge against his master.
- **Sources:** https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/throughtimeandspace.php ; https://primagames.com/news/witcher-3-final-age-main-quests-payback-final-preparations-through-time-and-space ; https://www.consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-through-time-and-space

### The endings (Something Ends, Something Begins) and how they are decided

- **Where and kind:** The epilogue of the main quest.
- **How it works:** There are three playable epilogues: Ciri as Empress, Ciri as a witcher, and Ciri's death ("Doubt, Death and Despair"). Ciri's survival is counted from five hidden reactions to how Geralt treats her: (1) a snowball fight after Vesemir's death, (2) what Geralt does about Emhyr's payment, (3) letting Ciri go to the Lodge of Sorceresses alone, (4) letting her wreck Avallac'h's lab ("Go for it"), (5) going with her to Skjall's grave. Guides say about three positive answers are needed for her to survive the White Frost. They disagree on which Emhyr answer counts as positive and on the exact maths. Whether she becomes Empress or a witcher depends on whether Geralt took her to see Emhyr _and_ on the war, which comes down to the side quest Reason of State (see below). The epilogue ends with slides of local and political consequences.
- **Scale:** Developers first spoke of 36 endings, then corrected that to 36 world states inside three epilogues, with hundreds of smaller variations ("stopped counting at 300"). Game Informer reports a script of about 450,000 words.
- **Devices:** A hidden score. Ordinary parenting moments, never shown as big choices, decide the ending. Side quests decide the fate of a war.
- **Sources:** https://www.gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/epilogue/something-ends-something-begins ; https://www.gamewatcher.com/2013-26-03-cd-projekt-red-reveals-three-playable-epilogues-for-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt ; https://www.engadget.com/2013-04-01-the-witcher-3-features-more-than-300-small-variations-in-its-end.html ; https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/the-witcher-3/witcher-3-endings-guide ; https://allthings.how/?p=228738 ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/292030/discussions/0/490125103635625688 ; https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2016/08/20/the-story-behind-the-story-of-the-witcher-3.aspx

## Secondary quests

### A Towerful of Mice

- **Where and kind:** Velen, Fyke Isle. Secondary quest, given by Keira Metz.
- **Premise:** Lift the curse on Fyke Isle's tower.
- **How it unfolds:** Keira's magic lamp shows ghostly apparitions and replays the last moments of those who died in the tower (finding them is optional). Clues show that the mage Alexander experimented on rats, infecting them with the plague called Catriona. The ghost Annabelle asks for help. **Choice:** help her, or refuse (if he refuses, she locks him in and attacks). Either way the curse can be broken. One route means fighting Annabelle as a pesta (plague maiden) and bringing her lover Graham to the island. On the other, Graham confesses his part in the tower's fall, and Annabelle shows herself as a plague maiden and kills him.
- **Consequences:** Both endings are tragic. A Filmstories analysis says the better ending goes to the player who investigated thoroughly and noticed the gaps in the spectre's own story. Finishing it leads Keira to ask another favour.
- **Devices:** The client (a ghost) is an unreliable witness, and the investigation is what shows it. A tool (the lamp) that replays past events. Pacing is called its strength.
- **Sources:** https://filmstories.co.uk/?p=83020 ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-a-towerful-of-mice-explained ; https://www.gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/velen/helping-keira-metz ; https://www.gamepressure.com/editorials/ranking-7-best-quests-in-the-witcher-3-on-its-7th-anniversary/a-towerful-of-mice/z5588 ; https://www.pcgamer.com/the-witcher-3s-best-quests

### For the Advancement of Learning, and Keira's fate (listed in the brief as "Now or Never")

- **Where and kind:** Velen. The end of Keira's chain of secondary quests. (The search couldn't place the Keira decision under "Now or Never"; it is in "For the Advancement of Learning". "Now or Never" is, _unverified_, Triss's departure quest in Novigrad.)
- **Premise:** Keira has notes on a cure for the Catriona plague and means to trade them to King Radovid for money and standing.
- **How it unfolds:** The dialogue has to draw out that she wants money and security before the option to steer her north appears; guides advise against aggressive lines. **Choices:** let her go to Radovid, persuade her to go to Kaer Morhen, or kill her. Keeping or confiscating the notes reportedly changes nothing later.
- **Consequences (delayed):** If she goes to Radovid, he has her impaled. That opens "A Final Kindness" in Novigrad (Triss asks Geralt to bring back Keira's body for burial), and a later main quest, "Brothers in Arms: Velen", fails because she isn't there for the Battle of Kaer Morhen. If she goes to Kaer Morhen, she fights in the battle, takes up with Lambert, and counts towards the "Full Crew" achievement.
- **Devices:** An ally won or lost by how you talk, which shows up in a much later battle. A death you hear about later, which opens a small quest of mourning.
- **Sources:** https://game8.co/games/Witcher3/archives/279454 ; https://primagames.com/eguides/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-eguide/epilogue-and-endings/about-main-quest-endings/character-endings ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-most-cursed-decisions ; https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/a-final-kindness/

### A Final Kindness

- **Where and kind:** Novigrad. A small secondary quest that exists only if Keira went to Radovid.
- **Premise:** Triss asks Geralt to bring back Keira's body after Radovid had her impaled.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt recovers the body, and Triss takes it for a proper burial.
- **Devices:** A quest that appears only because of an earlier choice in another region. The brief's "Pellar" link wasn't confirmed: one result mentions a Pellar body-burning errand in Crookback Bog, but no source named that quest.
- **Sources:** https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/a-final-kindness/ ; https://www.arrpeegeez.com/2016/02/the-witcher-3-walkthrough-side-quests_39.html

### Carnal Sins

- **Where and kind:** Novigrad. Secondary quest, after "Cabaret".
- **Premise:** Priscilla, a poet and Dandelion's friend, is attacked by a serial killer. Geralt investigates.
- **How it unfolds:** Her doctor, Joachim von Gratz, takes Geralt through the sewers to the morgue to examine the latest victim, a dwarf. Examining the head and torso shows the murders are ritual, with fire playing a part. A clue names Sweet Nettie as the next target. The obvious suspect, Reverend Nathaniel Pastodi, a priest of the Eternal Fire, is holding Nettie. **Choice:** attack Nathaniel at once, or question him first. Questioning him points to the coroner, Hubert Rejk, a higher vampire who kills in the name of the Eternal Fire. Geralt rescues Nettie and kills Rejk at the docks.
- **Consequences:** Attacking Nathaniel before questioning him ends the quest with the wrong man blamed and the real killer still free.
- **Devices:** A red herring, and an ending that punishes rushing to judgement. The killer is the person trusted to read the evidence.
- **Sources:** https://www.gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/secondary-quests/novigrad/carnal-sins ; https://www.thegamer.com/witcher-3-carnal-sins-quest-walkthrough/ ; https://segmentnext.com/the-witcher-3-carnal-sins/ ; https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Hubert_Rejk ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-cabaret-carnal-sins-questline

### A Matter of Life and Death

- **Where and kind:** Novigrad. Secondary quest, given by Triss, after "Count Reuven's Treasure".
- **Premise:** Triss is getting Novigrad's mages out of the city. The next to go is a young alchemist, Albert, Lady Vegelbud's son, smuggled out under cover of a masquerade.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt buys masks (a fox mask for Triss) at Elihal's shop. Wearing fine clothes earns a little XP and pleases Triss. At the ball, a tipsy Triss leads Geralt into a hedge maze. **Choice:** kiss her or not (this is what opens a romance with her). Witch hunters ambush Geralt, taking him for Albert, and the quest ends back with Triss after the stables.
- **Devices:** A social set piece around a rescue. A romance decided in a light moment. The disguise turns against the player when the hunters mistake him for the target.

### The Menge interrogation (Triss and the witch hunters)

### Reason of State

- **Where and kind:** Novigrad, Act III. Secondary quest with consequences for the main story. It opens only once several earlier quests (Roche's and Dijkstra's lines) are done. Starting "On Thin Ice" first makes it fail.
- **Premise:** Vernon Roche, Dijkstra and Thaler plot to kill King Radovid.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt helps with the plot, or stays out of it. After Radovid dies, Dijkstra turns on Roche's Temerians. **Choices:** "Do what you want" (stand aside and leave the Temerians to their fate), or "Won't let you kill them" (oppose Dijkstra and fight him).

- **Devices:** A side quest decides the war, and so the main ending. A coup whose winners turn on each other.

### The Last Wish

### Where the Cat and Wolf Play...

- **Where and kind:** Velen (Honorton). A secondary quest that grows out of the contract "The Beast of Honorton" (posted at Crow's Perch and Oreton).

### Gwent: Big City Players, and High Stakes

- **Where and kind:** Novigrad. Gwent card-game quests.

Dijkstra becomes unavailable during some later story quests. **High Stakes** is a tournament at the Passiflora, entered by paying the scribe; leaving the building mid-tournament fails it. ## Hearts of Stone

### Evil's Soft First Touches

- **Where and kind:** No Man's Land and Oxenfurt. The expansion's first main quest.
- **Premise:** A contract on the Seven Cats Inn board for a monster in the sewers. Geralt meets the client, Olgierd von Everec, at the Garin estate.
- **How it unfolds:** Olgierd's band may pick a fistfight, which the right reply avoids. The target is said to be a prince turned into a monster (the Toad Prince), and haggling raises the pay to 450 crowns. In the sewers under Oxenfurt, witcher senses track a key and the way down. Geralt meets Shani again and follows dead soldiers to the lair, and the boss fight is mandatory. _(Unverified: the contract is a trap. Killing the prince lands Geralt in the hands of the prince's Ofieri escort, and Gaunter O'Dimm frees him in return for serving Olgierd.)_ Geralt then meets "Master Mirror" at midnight at a crossroads under willows, and goes back to Olgierd. Leaving the estate opens the side quest "Rose on a Red Field".
- **Consequences:** The pact with O'Dimm frames the rest of the expansion: Geralt must grant Olgierd's three wishes. Reviewers sum it up as a monster contract that turns into a binding agreement between Geralt, Olgierd and O'Dimm.
- **Devices:** A routine contract that is a trap. A three-way pact. The estate burns, so what isn't looted is lost.
- **Sources:** https://gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/evilssoftfirsttouches.php ; https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Evil's+Soft+First+Touches ; https://gamerwalkthroughs.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/hearts-of-stone/evils-soft-first-touches ; https://gamesradar.com/witcher-3-hearts-stone-review

### Gaunter O'Dimm in the base game (foreshadowing)

- **Where and kind:** Prologue, White Orchard. Not a quest of its own.
- **What happens:** At the White Orchard inn (during "Lilac and Gooseberries"), a pleasant vagrant, Gaunter O'Dimm, tells Geralt where Yennefer was last seen, points him to the Nilfgaardian garrison and disappears. He returns as the antagonist of Hearts of Stone, also called "Master Mirror" or "Man of Glass".

### Olgierd's three wishes (structure)

- **Structure:** Olgierd sets three "impossible" wishes: show his dead brother Vlodimir "the time of his life"; get him a house (the Borsodi house); and a third, kept secret until the first two are done, which turns out to be his wife Iris's violet rose. The first two can be done in either order. The third ("Scenes from a Marriage") opens only after both.
- **Devices:** A list of tasks with a hidden last item. Each wish is a quest in a different genre (a comedy of possession, a heist, a ghost story).
- **Sources:** https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-hearts-of-stone-main-story-walkthrough ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-olgierd-von-everec-explained ; https://gamebanshee.com/k9aqx

### Dead Man's Party

- **Where and kind:** Hearts of Stone. Main quest (wish one).
- **Premise:** Show Olgierd's brother Vlodimir, long dead, a good time.
- **How it unfolds:** Shani has a tome that shows where the family crypt is. A blood ritual in the crypt summons the family's ghosts, who attack first. Then Vlodimir can be spoken to, and he possesses Geralt. Shani wakes Geralt, and the best place for Vlodimir's fun is the wedding she had invited Geralt to. Vlodimir runs Geralt's body through the wedding. He takes a liking to Shani but, learning what she means to Geralt, promises to keep his hands off. When he leaves, Geralt can stay and romance Shani.
- **Consequences:** Vlodimir's departure opens the optional "A Midnight Clear", which fails if Geralt leaves the wedding village first.
- **Devices:** The protagonist is possessed by a comic ghost, a change of tone in a dark story. A romance woven into the set piece. A tie-in quest you lose by leaving the area.
- **Sources:** https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Man%27s_Party ; https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Dead+Man's+Party ; https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/dead-mans-party ; https://www.gamebanshee.com/k9aqx ; https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/a-midnight-clear/

### Open Sesame! (the heist)

- **Where and kind:** Hearts of Stone, Oxenfurt. Main quest (wish two).
- **Premise:** Get Olgierd Maximilian Borsodi's house; Olgierd gives no location or means.
- **How it unfolds:** Vivaldi gets Geralt into the Borsodi auction house. Horst Borsodi throws him out, and he fights the guards outside. A stranger, Ewald Borsodi, the estranged brother, offers to rob the auction house and asks Geralt to assemble a crew and weaken the Redanian garrison. They meet at a herbalist's hut outside town, with a password. Three sub-quests recruit the crew, one of them "The Safecracker": **Quinto** (quiet: the vault opens while the guards outside fetch a cart) or **Casimir** (an explosion blows out the windows and the guards storm the house). In the vault, the brothers' feud comes into the open. **Choice:** side with Horst or with Ewald. Quinto sides with Horst and Casimir with Ewald, so Geralt may end up fighting his own safecracker. The fight ends when the brother Geralt is fighting goes down. Afterwards, take the whole haul or just the box (the deed). Then make a deal with Ewald for the house, or fight him for everything.
- **Consequences:** The main quest goes on either way. One review says the planning choices add flavour more than real freedom. Auction stock is lost if not bought then.
- **Devices:** A heist built by recruiting specialists, whose loyalties come out mid-job. The quest-giver has his own family grudge.
- **Sources:** https://witcher-games.fandom.com/wiki/Open_Sesame!_(Hearts_of_Stone) ; https://twinfinite.net/guides/the-witcher-3-hearts-of-stone-open-sesame-quest-guide/ ; https://gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/opensesame.php ; https://primagames.com/news/witcher-3-hearts-stone-open-sesame-quest-get-witcher-gear ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/reviews/the-witcher-3-hearts-of-stone-review

### Scenes from a Marriage

- **Where and kind:** Hearts of Stone. Main quest (wish three).
- **Premise:** Bring Olgierd the violet rose he once gave his wife Iris.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt goes into the von Everec manor and into a painted world built from Iris's memories and pain _(that the house's caretaker and the nightmare stages are walked through as memories is general knowledge, unverified)_. At the end, Geralt offers Iris a choice: stay in the world built from pain, or give up the rose that holds it together.
- **Consequences:** Taking the rose is part of the route that keeps the most story and leads to the best ending. A review says the choice "does not offer a clean rescue".
- **Devices:** A ghost story told as a walk through a marriage's memories. The "impossible" task means ending someone's afterlife.
- **Sources:** https://gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/scenesfromamarriage.php ; https://vulkk.com/2023/02/06/the-witcher-3-scenes-from-a-marriage-walkthrough/ ; https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Scenes+From+A+Marriage ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-iris-von-everec-explained

### Whatsoever a Man Soweth... (ending)

- **Where and kind:** Hearts of Stone. The final main quest.
- **Premise:** With the wishes granted, O'Dimm comes for Olgierd's soul.
- **How it unfolds:** An optional step: if Geralt sees Shani and Professor Shakeslock in Oxenfurt first, he learns O'Dimm can be beaten with a wager, because he is a gambler. Skip it and the good ending is closed. **Choice:** let O'Dimm take Olgierd, or challenge him. The challenge is a riddle in a dark otherworld against the clock: the answer is a reflection (O'Dimm is found in the water of a dry fountain once it fills, after Aard breaks a cracked wall).
- **Consequences:** If Geralt lets O'Dimm take the soul, O'Dimm offers a reward (5,000 crowns, a wish such as speed or a bottomless carafe, or where Ciri is). If Geralt wins, O'Dimm must vanish, and Olgierd gives him his sabre, Iris. The ending rewards are exclusive (the Viper silver sword on one route, Iris on the other).
- **Devices:** A devil's bargain beaten by information gathered earlier and optionally. A riddle as the final boss. A reward that tempts the player to pick the worse ending.
- **Sources:** https://witcher-games.fandom.com/wiki/Whatsoever_a_Man_Soweth%E2%80%A6 ; https://gamerwalkthroughs.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/hearts-of-stone/whatsoever-a-man-soweth/ ; https://allthings.how/?p=231203 ; https://www.thegamer.com/witcher-3-hearts-of-stone-whatsoever-a-man-soweth-guide/

## Blood and Wine

### The Beast of Toussaint (the Beast of Beauclair)

- **Where and kind:** Toussaint (Beauclair). Main quest that opens the expansion.
- **Premise:** Two knights, Milton de Peyrac-Peyran and Palmerin de Launfal, bring Geralt to Toussaint. Duchess Anna Henrietta hires him to stop a beast killing famous knights.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt investigates the victims (Prima's chapter title "Honor, Wisdom, Generosity, Valor, Compassion and Murder" points to a link with the knights' five virtues). The chase ends in a greenhouse, where Milton is found dead with the killer standing over him, and the killer escapes. The trail leads to a higher vampire, Dettlaff, an acquaintance of Geralt's old friend Regis. Regis calls Dettlaff more bestial than himself but no mindless killer. The investigation shows he acted out of rage, revenge and love: someone he loved was blackmailing him into the killings.
- **Consequences:** The blackmailer turns out to be Syanna (Sylvia Anna), the Duchess's exiled sister. The rest of the main line follows from that.
- **Devices:** The "beast" is a person being manipulated by another. The victims are linked in a way only investigation finds.
- **Sources:** https://gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/blood-and-wine-main-quests/the-beast-of-toussaint ; https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/The+Beast+of+Toussaint ; https://primagames.com/eguides/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-the-complete-edition-eguide/episode-2-main-quests-blood-and-wine/the-beast-of-toussaint/honor-wisdom-generosity-valor-compassion-and-murder ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-dettlaff-van-der-eretein-explained

### The Night of Long Fangs and Blood Simple

- **Where and kind:** Toussaint. Main quests in the middle of the expansion.
- **How it unfolds:** Dettlaff unleashes vampires on Beauclair (he had threatened a plague of beasts if Syanna wasn't handed over). Orianna, a bruxa, runs an orphanage, and helping her protect it opens the route to the Unseen Elder. In "Blood Simple" Geralt finds dead children and a survivor who blames a creature, later identified as an alpha garkain. The orphanage is revealed to be Orianna's blood supply. Geralt promises to come back for her but doesn't kill her within the expansion.
- **Devices:** The ally's charity is a front for predation. The reckoning is promised and never delivered (a fan mod later added one).
- **Sources:** https://gamerwalkthroughs.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/blood-and-wine/the-night-of-long-fangs/ ; https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/bloodsimple.php ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-orianna-bruxa-orphanage-explained

### La Cage au Fou, What Lies Unseen, and the route choice

- **Where and kind:** Toussaint. A main-quest branch.
- **How it unfolds:** There are two ways to reach Dettlaff: seek the Unseen Elder, the eldest vampire, who can compel him to come ("What Lies Unseen"), or go after Syanna through the Land of a Thousand Fables ("Beyond Hill and Dale..."). The choice isn't fixed at once: both leads can be followed for a while, and the Orianna path closes when Geralt tells Regis at the docks he wants the Unseen Elder. In "La Cage au Fou", Regis sacrifices something of his own to provide the last item needed at the Tesham Mutna ruins (details thin).
- **Consequences:** The Unseen Elder route is shorter, and guides say it locks the tragic ending: both Syanna and Anna Henrietta die.
- **Devices:** A fork that looks like a choice of method but decides the ending. The commitment point is a single line of dialogue to a friend.
- **Sources:** https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-choices-blood-and-wine ; https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/What+Lies+Unseen ; https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/reviews/the-witcher-3-blood-and-wine-review

### Beyond Hill and Dale... (the Land of a Thousand Fables)

- **Where and kind:** Toussaint, inside a magical fairy-tale world. Main quest.
- **Premise:** Find Syanna, hidden in an enchanted world of fairy tales.
- **How it unfolds:** Geralt crosses a world of fairy-tale set pieces (in Grandma's house, for instance, the Big Bad Wolf won't talk because Little Red Riding Hood is dead). The Little Flint Girl in the snowy village wears Syanna's ribbon, which can be had for 500 crowns or a game of Gwent. Diary entries in the playroom tell Syanna's childhood and banishment.
- **Consequences:** The ribbon and the diaries are what make the best ending possible. The ribbon later teleports Syanna to safety when Dettlaff strikes.
- **Devices:** An optional object, easy to miss and found in a whimsical side space, decides life and death in the finale. The villain's backstory is found as readable clues, which the player must read to sway her later.
- **Sources:** https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Beyond_Hill_and_Dale... ; https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Beyond+Hill+and+Dale... ; https://gamerwalkthroughs.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/blood-and-wine/beyond-hill-and-dale/ ; https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/how-to-get-the-best-ending-in-witcher-3-blood-and-wine/

### Tesham Mutna, Pomp and Strange Circumstance, and the endings

- **Where and kind:** Toussaint. The finale.
- **How it unfolds:** At Tesham Mutna, Dettlaff attacks Syanna. If Geralt has the ribbon, she vanishes to safety and Geralt (with Regis) must fight Dettlaff. A higher vampire can only truly be killed by another, so Regis finishes him. Without the ribbon, Dettlaff kills Syanna, and Geralt then chooses whether to fight or let him go. In "Pomp and Strange Circumstance", if Geralt has worked out that the Duchess was Syanna's last target, has read the diaries and heard Syanna out, he can persuade her to forgive Anna Henrietta.
- **Consequences:** **Best:** both sisters live and are reconciled (the Syanna route, with the ribbon, her story heard and the right dialogue). **Syanna dies:** Geralt is jailed (blamed for the Duchess's sister's death, and Dandelion can get him out). **Both die:** the Unseen Elder route, where Anna Henrietta dies at Syanna's hand. Guides disagree on some of this. A "most complete" route listed by one guide: spare the spotted wight, follow both leads during the vampire attack, enter the fairy-tale land, get the ribbon, investigate the fifth victim, persuade Syanna.
- **Devices:** Several small, optional, easily missed acts (an item, reading diaries, listening) add up to the best ending. A sympathetic killer whose death is required even in the best ending. A friend (Regis) pays a social price.

## How CD Projekt Red designs quests (what developers and critics have said)

- **The overall aim: choice and consequence in an open world.** Senior quest designer Philipp Weber, quoting quest director Mateusz Tomaszkiewicz, said the main goal was to combine the earlier Witcher games' "complex and mature story that has choices and consequences" with an open world. The challenge was quests that still work when players come at them by different routes. Weber said the change improved the quests: they became more open, and doing parts out of order could have later consequences, "a huge bonus". Source: https://www.gamespot.com/articles/cd-projekt-red-explains-why-the-witcher-3s-open-wo/1100-6477549/
- **No fetch quests, and every quest has a hook.** The studio head is reported not to want any "FedEx" or fetch quests. Every quest, "even the very tiny ones", is designed from scratch with "some kind of story, tease, twist, rumor". Writer Jakub Szamałek: "When we do have [fetch quests], which is rarely, we compensate for it with the narrative." Tomaszkiewicz admitted a few quests "even though we had a rule against it, felt padded; like fetch quests", which he blamed on ideas carried over from earlier games. For Hearts of Stone the team spent more time coming up with ideas from scratch. Philipp Weber says the rule carries on into the next game. Sources: https://comicbook.com/comicbook/news/the-witcher-3-developers-promise-you-wont-find-two-identical-sid/ ; https://stevivor.com/features/interviews/cd-projekt-reds-mateusz-tomaszkiewicz-on-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-and-lessons-learned-for-hearts-of-stone/ ; https://attackofthefanboy.com/news/cd-projekt-red-eliminating-fetch-quests-witcher-3/ ; https://gamingbolt.com/the-witcher-4-wont-have-fetch-quests-assures-narrative-director
- **Emotion as the goal.** Konrad Tomaszkiewicz told TechRaptor that CDPR was "all about evoking emotion in one form or another". Source: https://techraptor.net/content/tag/konrad-tomaszkiewicz
- **How a quest is made (the pipeline).** In a 2016 forum Q&A, Sasko and Mateusz Tomaszkiewicz described it: ideas come from writers (mostly for main quests) or from designers (most side quests). Designers write short pitches and the team trims the list. Chosen pitches are expanded into full scenarios and revised, or scrapped, over several passes. Then the designer lists the assets needed and builds a first draft with placeholders while other departments make the final assets, and keeps the quest's mood on track through lighting, music and animation. Writers outline what happens, and designers outline what the player does. For the Bloody Baron, Sasko designed and scripted the quest and Stachyra wrote the dialogue and cutscenes, with feedback from Tomaszkiewicz and story director Marcin Blacha. Sources: https://vulkk.com/2016/05/07/witcher-3-qa-cdprs-quest-designers/ ; https://www.shacknews.com/article/94509/witcher-3-quest-designers-talk-design-process-most-difficult-quest-to-create ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bloody_Baron ; https://pcgamer.com/how-the-witcher-3s-best-quest-was-made
- **Starting small.** The Bloody Baron began as a draft that could be "summed up with one sentence" (a Baron wants a monster killed and offers news of Ciri in return). Stachyra knew only that it would be about family and tied to Ciri. GamePro reports that Sasko said the design document ran to about 40 pages before a single line of dialogue was written. Sources: https://kotaku.com/the-story-behind-the-witcher-3s-bloody-baron-quest-1736090893 ; https://www.gamepro.de/artikel/the-witcher-3-quest-kein-dialog-in-40-seiten,3427812.html
- **Characters as mirrors, made sympathetic on purpose.** Sasko built the Baron to embody Velen and to mirror Geralt (two fathers who lost their families). An early, more violent version was toned down so he stayed sympathetic. Sources: https://pcgamer.com/how-the-witcher-3s-best-quest-was-made ; https://mein-mmo.de/witcher-3-blutiger-baron-hintergrund-interview-ausschnitt/
- **Hard choices with no obvious right answer.** Marcin Blacha (story director) has said a great quest should make the player stop and weigh the decision, the choice should never have an obvious right answer, and it should make players reflect on people, how the world works and how hard life can be. He said the same approach, including choices that may cause regret, carries into a coming expansion. Source: https://www.notebookcheck.net/The-Witcher-3-story-director-explains-what-makes-a-truly-great-quest.1400273.0.html
- **Ambiguity built in.** Game Informer reports Karolina Stachyra describing a story where "everything is ambiguous, and nothing's ever concrete", since everything can change with the player's decisions. It also reports a script of about 450,000 words and 36 world states. Source: https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2016/08/20/the-story-behind-the-story-of-the-witcher-3.aspx
- **Pacing, in hindsight.** Sasko later said "in the Bloody Baron quest, I made so many mistakes": it is almost four hours long, and he could have told the story better with better pacing. The rule the studio adopted while making the expansions: focused beats of about 40 minutes of intense, themed play and story, then open-world breathing room. The rule can be broken on purpose. Sources: https://kotaku.com/a-witcher-3-dev-reflects-on-its-most-memorable-quest-the-bloody-baron-quest-i-made-so-many-mistakes-2000730612 ; https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/09/03/432255-odin-iz-lucsix-kvestov-the-witcher-3-teper-smushhaet-razrabotcika-on-priznal-cto-dopustil-kucu-osibok.html
- **GDC 2023, "10 Key Quest Design Lessons" (Paweł Sasko).** The talk is organised around mastering the plot, structuring the narrative and improving the design. Lessons the summaries report: beware of overdesign ("a game is not a life simulation, it is entertainment"; details matter only when they carry meaning); hold back key information and let the player uncover it through effort, which keeps them moving towards the next story ("absence of information"); make affecting the player emotionally a core pillar and give each emotional scene time ("never rush"); experiment with topics nobody has done; be brief (cut dialogue you don't need). The full list wasn't readable in the search results. Sources: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/marketing/10-key-takeaways-from-the-quest-design-of-cyberpunk-2077-and-the-witcher-3 ; https://gdcvault.com/play/1028897/10-Key-Quest-Design-Lessons ; https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20230408-key-lessons-from-pawel-sasko ; https://www.gamebanshee.com/943k6 ; https://gamedevsjourney.substack.com/p/5-quest-design-lessons-from-cdpr
- **Killing a beloved character.** Sasko proposed Vesemir's death at the Battle of Kaer Morhen; the room went silent at first, and the team then decided it was the right step for Ciri's story. Both lead quest designers called the Battle of Kaer Morhen the hardest quest to make (many custom solutions and branches, from a thin outline). Sources: https://gamingbolt.com/the-witcher-3-lead-quest-designer-writes-about-bloody-baron-quest-line-killing-off-vesemir ; https://80.lv/articles/the-witcher-3-developer-on-hard-decisions-killing-important-characters/ ; https://www.gamewatcher.com/2016-05-05-the-witcher-3-s-battle-of-kaer-morhen-may-be-one-of-the-quest-designers-favourites-but-it-was-also-the-most-challenging-to-create ; https://www.gamepressure.com/editorials/interview-with-cd-projekt-reds-lead-quest-designer-pawel-sasko/zd661
- **A critic's method: mapping the "possibility space".** Filmstories' column "Of mice and Witchers: structuring quests" maps every element of A Towerful of Mice on a two-dimensional chart, arranged by how the elements relate. It stresses pacing, and notes that binary dialogue choices map cleanly, while working out which interactions enable new interactions in other systems is harder. PC Gamer describes the quest as starting with one goal and taking you through an unexpected slice of story. Sources: https://filmstories.co.uk/?p=83020 ; https://www.pcgamer.com/the-witcher-3s-best-quests
- **Critics on Hearts of Stone's structure.** Reviewers credit its focus to its length (about ten hours) and its range of tone ("celebrating at a wedding with a ghost... to planning a grand heist, to being genuinely terrified"). PC Gamer found the heist largely driven by cutscenes and the bosses tactically simple. Sources: https://gamesradar.com/witcher-3-hearts-stone-review ; https://www.pcgamer.com/au/the-witcher-3-hearts-of-stone-review ; https://opencritic.com/game/1827/view/reviews
