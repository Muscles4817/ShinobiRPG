# Naruto arcs and missions: research notes

Scope and method. This is a data-gathering pass on how Naruto's story arcs and missions are put
together (who orders them, how they turn, what they leave behind), meant for a later pass on
structures for Shinobi RPG's arcs. It covers 29 arcs, missions and films: Part I canon and filler,
Part II (Shippūden) canon and filler, three films, and a closing section on mission ranks and small
jobs. Sources: Narutopedia (naruto.fandom.com) arc, episode, chapter and character pages, plus a few
plot-summary sites. **Caveat on method:** in this environment WebFetch could not load any page.
naruto.fandom.com answered HTTP 402 (also through its api.php), and every other host (Wikipedia,
MyAnimeList, mirrors, summary sites) failed DNS lookup. So everything here comes from WebSearch
results (searches limited to naruto.fandom.com where possible), which return summaries of the
pages listed under each entry. Those listed URLs were returned by search; none were opened directly.
Where search summaries contradicted each other or seemed garbled, the entry says so. A few
well-known canon details that searches did not confirm come from general knowledge of the series
and are marked "(not confirmed in sources)". Episode and chapter ranges come from the wiki
summaries; ranges marked "approx." are from general knowledge.

## Part I canon

### Survival training (the bell test)

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 4–8, episodes 4–5. Canon. Not a ranked mission: it is the jōnin's
  test of whether a new team stays together.
- **Premise:** Kakashi tells his three new genin to take one of two bells from him by noon. Anyone
  without a bell gets no lunch (he told them to skip breakfast) and goes back to the Academy.
- **Beats:** Each genin attacks alone and fails. Naruto tries to eat the lunches early and is tied
  to a post. Kakashi scolds all three for no teamwork, then gives a second chance: the other two may
  eat, Naruto may not, and anyone who feeds him fails. Sasuke and Sakura feed him anyway.
- **Twist:** The two bells are a trap meant to set them against each other. The real test is
  whether they put the team ahead of the rules, and breaking the "don't feed him" rule is what
  passes them.
- **Leaves behind:** Team 7 is formed, and the team's guiding rule is set: abandoning comrades is
  worse than breaking orders. Later arcs come back to it.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_(Kakashi),
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team-work_is_Outstanding

### Land of Waves (Prologue — Land of Waves)

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 9–33, episodes 6–19 (the wiki counts the arc from chapter 1 /
  episode 1). Canon. Started as C-rank, really B-rank or higher.
- **Premise:** Naruto, tired of D-rank chores, demands a real mission. The Third Hokage gives Team 7
  a C-rank escort: guard the bridge builder Tazuna on his way home and while he works.
- **The lie:** Tazuna asked for protection from bandits. Two Mist chūnin (the Demon Brothers) ambush
  them on the road, which proves ninja are after him. Tazuna admits the job is really a B-rank one:
  the shipping magnate Gatō has strangled the Land of Waves, the country is too poor to pay for
  higher-ranked protection, and the bridge to the mainland would break Gatō's hold. The team chooses
  to go on anyway.
- **Escalation:** Gatō hires the missing-nin Zabuza Momochi, once one of the Seven Swordsmen of the
  Mist. Kakashi beats him at the lake but a masked "hunter-nin" (Haku) takes Zabuza away, faking his
  death. During the recovery week the team trains (tree climbing), Naruto meets Haku without
  knowing who he is, and Tazuna's grandson Inari, who lost his stepfather to Gatō, says resisting is
  pointless.
- **Climax:** On the unfinished bridge Haku fights Naruto and Sasuke and seems to kill Sasuke.
  Haku throws himself in front of Kakashi's Lightning Cutter to save Zabuza and dies. Gatō arrives
  with hired thugs, says he never meant to pay Zabuza, and insults Haku's body. Naruto shames
  Zabuza; Zabuza kills Gatō with a kunai and dies beside Haku. Inari brings the villagers to the
  bridge, and Gatō's men run.
- **Moral grey:** The antagonists are sympathetic: Haku is a tool by choice, devoted to the man who
  took him in, and Zabuza grieves him in the end. The real villain is the employer, who betrays his
  own hired blades.
- **Leaves behind:** The bridge is named the Great Naruto Bridge. Naruto's way of the ninja (never
  go back on his word) is set. Zabuza's sword and Haku's story return in Part II.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_%E2%80%94_Land_of_Waves,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A_Dangerous_Mission!_Journey_to_the_Land_of_Waves!,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Weapons_Known_as_Shinobi,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Haku, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zabuza_Momochi,
  https://www.sportskeeda.com/naruto/tazuna-in-naruto, https://mvlteenvoice.com/tag/naruto

### Chūnin Exams

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 34–115, episodes 20–67 (approx.). Canon. An exam held jointly by
  allied villages, not a mission.
- **Premise:** The jōnin senseis nominate their teams. Teams of three must enter together.
- **First exam (written test):** The proctor Ibiki Morino, an interrogation expert, gives an
  impossible paper where cheating is tolerated as long as you aren't caught (the earlier questions
  test information-gathering). The tenth question comes with a threat: skip it and your whole team
  fails; answer wrong and you may never sit the exam again. Many quit. Naruto refuses to, and steadies
  the room. There is no tenth question: choosing to stay is the answer.
- **Second exam (Forest of Death):** Each team gets a Heaven or an Earth scroll and must take the
  other from a rival team, then reach the tower in the middle within five days, all three alive.
  Anko finds the bodies of three Grass genin; one face was stolen. "Shiore" is Orochimaru, who
  corners Team 7, puts the Cursed Seal of Heaven on Sasuke and sends his Sound genin after him.
  Kabuto, a friendly Leaf genin who helps the team, turns out to be Orochimaru's spy (not confirmed
  in sources).
- **Preliminaries:** Too many teams pass, so one-on-one bouts thin them. Neji nearly kills his cousin
  Hinata (main versus branch house of the Hyūga). Gaara crushes Rock Lee.
- **Third exam (finals):** A month to train. Naruto beats Neji, breaking his belief in fate (the
  branch house's "caged bird"). Shikamaru outthinks Temari, then forfeits. Sasuke arrives late.
- **Leaves behind:** Promotion is judged on conduct, not wins: only Shikamaru is made chūnin
  (promotion not confirmed in sources). Orochimaru's interest in Sasuke, the cursed seal, Neji's
  change and Gaara's grudge all carry on. The exam itself is the cover for the next arc.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_(Arc),
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ch%C5%ABnin_Exam_Stage_2:_The_Forest_of_Death,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_of_Heaven,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A_Failure's_True_Power,
  https://daddyjim.ai/naruto/episode/The-Tenth-Question-All-or-Nothing

### Konoha Crush

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 116–138, episodes 68–80. Canon.
- **Premise:** The exam finals were the cover for an invasion by Sound and Sand, an ally of the
  Leaf.
- **Beats:** At a signal during the finals, Sand and Sound shinobi attack, and giant snakes break
  the walls. The "Fourth Kazekage" sitting beside the Third Hokage takes him hostage. It is
  Orochimaru, who murdered the real Kazekage and took his place (from the Rice Fields page). The
  Sound Four raise a barrier around the two Kage. Orochimaru brings back the First and Second
  Hokage; the Third uses the Dead Demon Consuming Seal, taking the dead Hokage's souls and then
  Orochimaru's arms, and dies.
- **Parallel thread:** Gaara, the Sand's jinchūriki (the One-Tails), is hurt and pulled out of the
  fight. Sasuke chases him. Gaara turns into the Shukaku. Naruto summons Gamabunta and wins with a
  headbutt. Gaara realises Naruto's strength comes from protecting people.
- **The turn:** The Sand was a pawn: Orochimaru tricked it into the war by killing its Kage while
  wearing his face. The invasion falls apart when the Third dies and Gaara loses.
- **Leaves behind:** The Hokage is dead (a funeral, and the search for a successor). Orochimaru
  can't use his arms and needs a new body (Sasuke). The Sand repairs relations with the Leaf (not
  confirmed in sources). Gaara begins to change.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_(Arc),
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush,_Ended!!,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zero_Hour!_The_Konoha_Crush_Begins!,
  https://daddyjim.ai/naruto/arc/Konoha-Crush-Arc

### Search for Tsunade

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 139–171, episodes 81–100 (approx.). Canon. Rated B-rank by the wiki.
- **Premise:** The council asks Jiraiya to be Hokage. He refuses and names Tsunade, who left the
  village years ago. He sets off to find her with Naruto, promising to teach him a technique.
- **Beats:** Itachi and Kisame (Akatsuki, first time on screen) slip into the Leaf, beat Asuma and
  Kurenai and put Kakashi in a coma with Tsukuyomi. They are after the Nine-Tails in Naruto, and
  Jiraiya stops them at an inn. Sasuke, chasing his brother, is beaten and broken again. On the road
  Naruto learns the Rasengan. They find Tsunade, a gambler, in Tanzaku Town (not confirmed in
  sources).
- **The other offer:** Orochimaru reached Tsunade first. He wants his arms healed and offers to bring
  back her brother Nawaki and her lover Dan. She is tempted and gives herself a week.
- **The wager:** Tsunade bets Naruto can't master the Rasengan in a week: the First Hokage's necklace
  against his wallet. The necklace is "cursed"; the two she gave it to both died, and since then she
  is afraid of blood.
- **Climax:** Tsunade nearly heals Orochimaru, then attacks him instead. Kabuto takes Naruto on, and
  Naruto lands a Rasengan. Tsunade overcomes her fear of blood to shield Naruto. The three Sannin
  summon and fight; Orochimaru withdraws.
- **Leaves behind:** Tsunade gives Naruto the necklace and becomes the Fifth Hokage, then heals
  Kakashi and Lee. Akatsuki's hunt for jinchūriki is in the open. Sasuke's grudge deepens.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Search_for_Tsunade,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inheritance!_The_Necklace_of_Death!,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Hokage's_Necklace,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Wager%E2%80%A6!!, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade,
  https://daddyjim.ai/naruto/arc/Search-for-Tsunade

### Sasuke Recovery Mission

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 172–238, episodes 107–135. Canon. A-rank (rank not confirmed in
  sources).
- **Premise:** Sasuke leaves the village to go to Orochimaru, escorted by the Sound Four. With the
  jōnin away, Tsunade gives the newly made chūnin Shikamaru the job of building a squad of genin to
  bring him back.
- **Setup beats:** Sasuke's rooftop fight with Naruto, broken up by Kakashi, and the Sound Four's
  promise of power push him over the line. Sakura begs him to stay, then begs Naruto to bring him
  home; he promises (the "promise of a lifetime").
- **Structure:** A relay. Shikamaru recruits Naruto, Chōji, Kiba (with Akamaru) and Neji (Lee is
  recovering from surgery). At each Sound Four member, one member stays behind to fight so the rest
  can go on: Chōji against Jirōbō, Neji against Kidōmaru, Kiba against Sakon and Ukon, Shikamaru
  against Tayuya. Kimimaro takes Sasuke's pod ahead; Lee arrives and holds him. Most of the squad
  are near death until the Sand siblings, sent as reinforcements, arrive. Kimimaro dies of his
  illness mid-fight.
- **Climax:** Naruto catches Sasuke at the Valley of the End. Sasuke has taken the drug that raises
  his cursed seal to level two; Naruto draws on the Nine-Tails' cloak. Sasuke awakens the full
  Sharingan, wins, spares Naruto and goes on.
- **Ending:** The mission fails. Every member is badly hurt. Naruto apologises to Sakura for
  breaking his promise; she thanks him for trying. Naruto leaves to train with Jiraiya for about
  three years.
- **Leaves behind:** The unkept promise drives all of Part II. The Sand, enemies a few months
  earlier, are allies now.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Team,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Formation!_The_Sasuke_Recovery_Team,
  https://daddyjim.ai/naruto/arc/Sasuke-Recovery-Mission,
  https://criticalhits.com.br/anime/naruto-tudo-sobre-o-arco-da-missao-de-recuperacao-de-sasuke

### Kakashi Gaiden (Kannabi Bridge)

- **Where:** Canon side story, chapters 239–244, set about twenty years earlier during the Third
  Shinobi World War, published between Part I and Part II. Animated in Shippūden episodes 119–120.
- **Premise:** Kakashi has just been made jōnin. Minato sends his team (Kakashi, Obito, Rin) to
  destroy the Kannabi Bridge behind Iwagakure's lines, with Kakashi in command while Minato fights
  the main force.
- **Beats:** Kakashi puts the mission above comrades, by the rules his father was shamed for
  breaking. Rin is taken by Iwa ninja. Obito goes after her against orders; Kakashi follows. Kakashi
  loses his left eye and Obito awakens his Sharingan. They free Rin, a cave-in crushes Obito, and he
  gives Kakashi his left Sharingan as the promotion gift he had forgotten. Minato arrives and the
  bridge is destroyed.
- **Leaves behind:** Kakashi's creed (those who abandon comrades are worse than trash), his borrowed
  eye and the name "Copy Ninja". Obito's "death" sets up the series' biggest reveal.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Gaiden,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Minato, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Side_Story_1:_Mission_Start%E2%80%A6!!

## Part I filler (anime only)

### Land of Tea Escort Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 102–106. Filler.
- **Premise:** Team 7 is hired to escort a runner for the Wasabi family in the Land of Tea's
  dangerous annual race, because the rival Wagarashi family is said to have hired ninja to
  sabotage it. The race decides which family gets the port's business.
- **Beats:** The Wasabi runner is Idate Morino, who turns out to be Ibiki's younger brother and a
  former Leaf genin who fled the village. The race runs over sea and islands with sabotage at each
  stage. The antagonist, Aoi Rokushō, was Idate's teacher and the reason he ran from the Leaf: he
  manipulated him into a betrayal (not confirmed in sources). Aoi fights with the stolen Sword of the
  Thunder God.
- **Ending:** Idate wins the race. The corrupt official behind the Wagarashi has to become a monk,
  and the Wagarashi family is broken up. Idate makes peace with his past (sources disagree on whether
  he stays in the Land of Tea).
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Tea_Escort_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Idate_Morino,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Run_Idate_Run!_Nagi_Island_Awaits!

### Land of Rice Fields Investigation Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 136–141. Filler (English dub: Land of Rice Paddies).
- **Premise:** Jiraiya takes Naruto and Sakura to the Land of Rice Fields (home of the Sound
  village) to gather information on Orochimaru and Sasuke.
- **Beats:** In an outlaw town they meet Sasame of the Fūma clan, looking for her cousin Arashi, who
  went missing with most of the clan. Sasame leads them to an Orochimaru hideout, where the team
  splits up. A "Kabuto" turns out to be the Fūma kunoichi Kagerō in disguise; she dies after warning
  Sasame how much Arashi has changed.
- **The turn:** The Fūma joined Orochimaru for a promise of restoring their clan. Arashi was fused
  with two other clan members by an unstable technique and driven mad.
- **Ending:** Naruto, Sasame and Sakura bring Arashi back to himself. Sasuke is not found, so the
  investigation fails, but the clan is freed from Orochimaru. Hanzaki, a Fūma who refused the
  bargain, takes in Sasame to rebuild the clan.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Rice_Fields_Investigation_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasame, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kager%C5%8D,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hanzaki

### Mizuki Tracking Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 142–147. Filler.
- **Premise:** Mizuki, the chūnin who tricked Naruto into stealing the forbidden scroll in episode
  1, breaks out of the Leaf's prison with two other inmates (Fūjin and Raijin). Naruto and Iruka go
  after him.
- **Beats:** Naruto learns of a link to Orochimaru. Mizuki's former lover Tsubaki helps them find his
  hideout, where he has instructions for a strength-enhancing drug. He takes it and turns into a
  beast-like form; Naruto recognises it as close to Orochimaru's cursed seal.
- **Twist:** The seal is a failed prototype (the anime's Animal Cursed Seal). It burns him out: the
  more chakra he puts into strength, the slower he gets. Orochimaru was using him as a test subject.
- **Ending:** Naruto and Iruka beat him with a Rasengan. Questioned, he knows nothing of Orochimaru's
  whereabouts and goes back to prison. The arc closes the series' first villain and the first
  Naruto-Iruka bond.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki_Tracking_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Three_Villains_from_the_Maximum_Security_Prison

### Bikōchū Search Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 148–151. Filler.
- **Premise:** Shino says a rare bikōchū beetle could track Sasuke if it smelled his scent. Kurenai
  is in hospital after the prison break, so Shino leads Team 8, with Naruto as a fourth member.
- **Beats:** The Kamizuru clan of Iwagakure, bee users, also want the beetle to restore their clan's
  honour. Hinata finds it with her Byakugan and Naruto catches it. Hinata is abducted and held for the
  egg; she escapes and uses a new technique to save the team.
- **Ending:** The mission fails by accident: the hatchling memorises Naruto's scent (he breaks wind
  near it) instead of Sasuke's, and a bikōchū memorises only one scent in its life. The rival clan
  is beaten.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bik%C5%8Dch%C5%AB_Search_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bik%C5%8Dch%C5%AB,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A_Battle_of_Bugs!_The_Deceivers_and_the_Deceived!

### Kurosuki Family Removal Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 152–157. Filler.
- **Premise:** Three injured men from the Katabami Gold Mine village ask the Leaf for help against
  the gang terrorising them. Tsunade sends Naruto with Team Guy, partly to keep him from obsessing
  over Sasuke and Jiraiya. Naruto hopes the gang's leader, a former Mist Swordsman like Kisame,
  will know something.
- **Beats:** The villagers are so afraid that they refuse to be rescued. The leader, Raiga Kurosuki,
  has a mad love of funerals and buries people alive. Much of his power comes from Ranmaru, a sickly
  boy with a rare dōjutsu who rides on his back.
- **Moral grey:** Ranmaru is not a villain: he has stayed with Raiga for years by choice, because
  they understood each other. When Raiga is dying, Ranmaru gives his own strength to save him.
- **Ending:** Once Ranmaru chooses another life, Raiga gives himself his own "funeral" with his
  lightning. Ranmaru stays behind as an apprentice (to Sanshō, per the wiki) and the team goes home.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurosuki_Family_Removal_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Funeral_March_for_the_Living,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raiga's_Counterattack

### Star Guard Mission (Hoshigakure)

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 178–183. Filler.
- **Premise:** Team Guy is sent to guard the "star" of Hoshigakure, a meteorite; Naruto comes along
  because he suspects Orochimaru wants it.
- **Beats:** Sumaru, a young genin of the village, guides them past its poison gases. The star's
  radiation strengthens chakra and makes a special training possible, and that training is harming
  the village's young ninja. The acting Hoshikage, Akahoshi, killed the Third Hoshikage and restarted
  it.
- **Twist:** Natsuhi, Sumaru's mother, secretly finished the training and stole the star to stop the
  harm. Akahoshi takes Sumaru hostage to force her to give it up and later kills her.
- **Ending:** Akahoshi fuses the star into his body; Naruto destroys it with a Rasengan. The
  afflicted Hoshi ninja are treated in the Leaf. The village loses its source of power and keeps its
  children.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Guard_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Natsuhi, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akahoshi,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sumaru

### Yakumo Kurama Rescue Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 203–207. Filler.
- **Premise:** Kurenai leaves Team 8 for a secret task: dealing with an old student, Yakumo Kurama,
  whose genjutsu is so strong it can do real harm, even kill. Lightning strikes the Hokage's office,
  and Naruto follows.
- **Beats:** Yakumo's uncle Unkai leads clan members against her. The Kurama village was never
  destroyed: it was a vast genjutsu of Yakumo's. Without training, her bloodline grew a second,
  murderous personality, Ido.
- **The grey:** The Third Hokage and Kurenai decided her power had to go before Ido took over, and
  Kurenai sealed it, so the "rescuer" is also the one who crippled her. Yakumo blames Kurenai for her
  parents' death.
- **Ending:** Naruto tells Yakumo the truth about her parents' death, which frees Ido. Kurenai helps
  her face it, and she defeats it. Kurenai goes back to Team 8.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yakumo_Kurama_Rescue_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yakumo_Kurama,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurenai's_Top_Secret_Mission:_The_Promise_with_the_Third_Hokage

### Gantetsu Escort Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 209–212. Filler.
- **Premise:** Naruto, Sakura and Lee help transfer a prisoner, Gantetsu, a ninja dropout of the
  Shinobazu gang, to prison, and must guard him from his former comrades led by Shura.
- **Beats:** The guard captain, Todoroki, wants revenge on Gantetsu for the death of his family five
  years ago (search summaries garble who killed whom). On the road Gantetsu shows he has changed:
  he cares for orphans. The Shinobazu attack in the Forest of Bewilderment.
- **Ending:** The Shinobazu are defeated. Gantetsu ends up living peacefully in the forest with the
  orphans; the guard's revenge is set aside.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gantetsu_Escort_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Enemy:_Ninja_Dropouts,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_of_Bewilderment

## Part II canon (Shippūden)

### Kazekage Rescue Mission

- **Where:** Part II, chapters 245–281 (volumes 28–32), Shippūden episodes 1–32 (approx.). Canon.
- **Premise:** Gaara, now Kazekage, is taken by Akatsuki's Deidara and Sasori for the One-Tails.
  Team Kakashi (Naruto, Sakura, Kakashi) is sent to the Sand to help; Team Guy follows.
- **Beats:** Deidara gets into the Sand with help from Sasori's sleeper agent, Yūra, a trusted Sand
  official. Kankurō goes after them and is poisoned by Sasori. Sakura draws out the poison and makes
  antidotes, impressing the old puppeteer Chiyo, Sasori's grandmother, who joins the rescue.
- **Delays:** Akatsuki's leader sends copies of Itachi and Kisame to hold off both teams. They are
  stand-ins, and the fights only buy time for the three-day extraction.
- **Climax:** At the lair, Gaara is already dead. Naruto blames Chiyo for making him a jinchūriki.
  Sakura and Chiyo fight Sasori; his katana poisons Sakura, Chiyo saves her, and they kill him.
  Sasori, dying, reveals a meeting with his spy in Orochimaru's camp. Naruto and Kakashi chase
  Deidara, who gets away.
- **Ending:** Chiyo gives her life to revive Gaara, and Sand shinobi run out to greet him: the boy
  the village feared now has its love. Chiyo's funeral.
- **Leaves behind:** Sasori's spy meeting starts the next arc. Chiyo's change of heart about
  jinchūriki. Deidara's grudge against Sasuke.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage_Rescue_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Return_of_the_Kazekage, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasori,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deidara, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_vs._Itachi!!

### Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission

- **Where:** Part II, chapters 282–310, Shippūden episodes 33–53 (approx.). Canon. Anime title
  "Long-Awaited Reunion".
- **Premise:** With Kakashi in hospital, Team Kakashi is rebuilt under Yamato with a new member,
  Sai, a Root agent placed there by Danzō. The job: go to the Tenchi Bridge in the Land of Grass in
  place of the dead Sasori and meet his spy in Orochimaru's camp.
- **Beats:** Yamato goes disguised as Sasori. The spy is Kabuto, who may be double-crossing both
  sides. Orochimaru arrives and the meeting turns into a fight; Naruto loses control of the
  Nine-Tails and hurts Sakura.
- **Twist:** Sai goes over to Orochimaru, carrying a letter from Danzō. Yamato tracks him by a seed

### Hidan and Kakuzu (Akatsuki Suppression Mission)

### Itachi Pursuit Mission

- **Where:** Part II, chapters approx. 343–404, Shippūden episodes 113–143 (approx.). Canon.
- **Premise:** Sasuke has killed (absorbed) Orochimaru and formed Hebi with three freed prisoners
  (Suigetsu, Karin, Jūgo) to hunt Itachi. The Leaf sends an eight-man squad (Team Kakashi and Team 8)
  to find him through Itachi.
- **The rule of the hunt:** Kakashi reasons that Itachi must be caught, not killed, since he leads to
  Sasuke, which is why he recruits Team 8's trackers.
- **Beats:** Sasuke fights Deidara, who blows himself up; Kiba picks up Sasuke's scent from the
  blast. Tobi stalls the squad. Sasuke and Itachi fight at the Uchiha hideout; Itachi dies with a
  smile, poking Sasuke's forehead.
- **The reveal:** Tobi, calling himself Madara Uchiha, tells Sasuke that Itachi was a double agent:
  the village elders ordered him to kill his clan to stop a coup; he spared Sasuke, was terminally
  ill, and lived so Sasuke could kill him and come home a hero.
- **Ending:** The Leaf squad arrives too late; Tobi takes Sasuke. Sasuke renames his team Taka and
  makes destroying the Leaf his goal, working with Akatsuki.
- **Moral grey:** The villain was the hero, the village the culprit. The revenge the hero lived for
  turns into revenge on the hero's own side.

### Pain's Assault

- **Where:** Part II, chapters approx. 420–453, Shippūden episodes 152–175 (approx.). Canon.
- **Premise:** Jiraiya dies spying on Akatsuki's leader, Pain, in Amegakure (the preceding chapters),

  sage mode on Mount Myōboku.
- **Beats:** Pain's six bodies split up and search the village; the Leaf's shinobi win some fights
  but cannot stop the destruction. Kakashi dies against Pain. Pain flattens the village with one
  Shinra Tensei. Tsunade exhausts herself protecting the villagers and falls into a coma, and Danzō
  takes over as acting Hokage.

  loves him and is struck down; Naruto loses control towards the Nine-Tails and is pulled back by
  his father Minato's sealed chakra.

  Jiraiya with Konan and Yahiko, and who seeks peace through shared pain. Naruto refuses to kill him,
  saying he will break the cycle of revenge. Nagato uses his last strength to bring back everyone he
  killed, and dies.

## Mission ranks and small missions

### The rank scale (Narutopedia "Missions" and rank pages)

- **D-rank:** Lowest; given to genin, often not real ninja work (finding pets, weeding a garden).
  Pay 5,000–50,000 ryō.
- **C-rank:** Genin or chūnin. Guarding people against bandits or highwaymen, background
  investigations, catching bandits or thieves, dealing with wild animals. Pay 30,000–100,000 ryō.
  Fighting enemy ninja is not expected.
- **B-rank:** Chūnin or jōnin; enemy ninja are expected. Pay 80,000–200,000 ryō (the wikis
  disagree: Portuguese 80,000–120,000, German up to 200,000).
- **A-rank:** Jōnin; can affect a village or country (escorting VIPs, fighting enemy squads).
  Pay 150,000–1,000,000 ryō (German and Polish wikis).
- **S-rank:** Highest and most dangerous, only for highly skilled jōnin or large squads (state
  secrets, assassinating a Kage, a coup). Pay at least 1,000,000 ryō.
- **Currency:** 1 ryō is about 10 yen.
- **How it is used in the story:** The rank is set by what the client says the danger is, and the
  client pays for it (Tazuna's under-ranking is the Land of Waves hook). Missions are handed out at
  the Mission Assignment Desk by the Hokage and Academy chūnin (Iruka objects to Team 7 being given a
  C-rank too early).
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Missions, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/D-rank,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/S-rank, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ry%C5%8D,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_Assignment_Desk

### Small missions shown in the series

- **Catching Tora (D-rank, episode 6):** Team 7 tracks down Tora, the lost cat of the Fire Daimyō's
  wife Madam Shijimi; the cat claws Naruto and runs from its owner's smothering hug. Tora runs away
  again and again; later Team Ebisu (Konohamaru's team) is hired to catch the same cat, a running
  gag that passes the job to the next generation.
- **The other D-ranks offered that day:** the Third Hokage lists babysitting the chief counsellor's
  three-year-old, shopping for a neighbour, and helping dig potatoes (anime, episode 6; only
  babysitting confirmed in sources). Naruto refuses and demands a real job.
- **D-rank versus a C-rank compromise (Shippūden):** Shizune suggests walking someone's dog, since
  Naruto, as a jinchūriki, shouldn't leave the village; Tsunade compromises with a C-rank escort of a
  gold convoy (per the "Run, Kankurō" page; wiki places it at Shippūden episode 7, which the
  summary may have mixed up).
- **First C-rank (Land of Waves):** a bodyguard job against bandits, which the client under-ranked.
  See above.
- **Training as missions:** Chūnin Exam stages, the bell test and the Rasengan wager all work as
  jobs with stated terms and a hidden real test (teamwork, nerve, conduct).
