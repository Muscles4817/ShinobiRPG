# Naruto arcs and missions: research notes

Scope and method. This is a data-gathering pass on how Naruto's story arcs and missions are put
together (who orders them, how they turn, what they leave behind), meant for a later pass on
structures for Shinobi RPG's arcs. It covers 29 arcs, missions and films: Part I canon and filler,
Part II (Shippūden) canon and filler, three films, and a closing section on mission ranks and small
jobs. **Method:** the first draft was written from web-search summaries only, because page fetches
were blocked. In a second pass every entry was checked against the pages themselves: Narutopedia
(naruto.fandom.com) wikitext fetched through the wiki's MediaWiki API (the arc page for every
entry, plus the episode, chapter, character, mission-rank and technique pages listed under each
entry), and Wikipedia's "Naruto season 1" episode list. Every URL under Sources is a page that was
opened in that pass; the summary sites the first draft cited were dropped. Episode and chapter
ranges are the arc pages' own. Where a page truly lacks a detail it is still marked
"(not confirmed in sources)". Narutopedia mixes manga and anime; where they differ, the entry says
which it follows.

## Part I canon

### Survival training (the bell test)

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 4–8, episodes 4–5, inside the wiki's "Prologue — Land of Waves" arc.
  Canon. Not a ranked mission: it is the jōnin's test of whether a new team stays together.
- **Premise:** Kakashi tells his three new genin to take one of two bells from him by noon. Anyone
  without a bell gets no lunch (he told them to skip breakfast), and with only two bells at least
  one of them goes back to the Academy.
- **Beats:** Each genin attacks alone and fails. Naruto tries to eat the lunches early and is tied
  to a post. Kakashi scolds all three for no teamwork, then gives a second chance: the other two may
  eat, Naruto may not, and anyone who feeds him fails. Sasuke and Sakura feed him anyway, and when
  Kakashi appears furious they say that if one of them fails, they all fail.
- **Twist:** The two bells are a trap meant to set them against each other. The real test is
  teamwork, and breaking the "don't feed him" rule is what passes them. Kakashi has never passed a
  team before.
- **Leaves behind:** Team 7 is formed, and the team's guiding rule is set: "those who don't follow
  the rules are scum, but those who abandon their friends are worse than scum". Later arcs come
  back to it.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_%E2%80%94_Land_of_Waves,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pass_or_Fail:_Survival_Test,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/You_Failed!_Kakashi's_Final_Decision,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_(Kakashi)

### Land of Waves (Prologue — Land of Waves)

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 9–33, episodes 6–19 (the wiki's arc runs from chapter 1 / episode 1).
  Canon. Given as C-rank; Kakashi says it would have been B-rank or higher, and the databook the
  wiki cites rates it "actually an A-rank".
- **Premise:** After a D-rank (catching a lost cat), Naruto demands a real mission. The Third
  Hokage gives Team 7 a C-rank escort: take the bridge builder Tazuna home to the Land of Waves.
- **The lie:** Tazuna asked only for an escort against bandits. Two Mist chūnin-level ninja (the
  Demon Brothers) ambush them on the road, which proves ninja are after him. Kakashi points out that
  had Tazuna said so, the job would have been B-rank or higher and gone to another team. Tazuna
  explains: the shipping magnate Gatō has bankrupted the Land of Waves, the country can't afford
  proper protection, and the bridge to the mainland would break Gatō's monopoly. Naruto, poisoned
  in the ambush, bleeds the poison out with a kunai and vows never to back down, and the team goes
  on.
- **Escalation:** Gatō has hired the missing-nin Zabuza Momochi, once one of the Seven Ninja
  Swordsmen of the Mist (the Demon Brothers are his men). Kakashi beats him at the lake, but a
  masked "hunter-nin" (Haku) takes Zabuza away with senbon, faking his death. During the recovery
  week the team trains (tree climbing), Naruto meets Haku in the woods without knowing who he is,
  and Tazuna's grandson Inari, whose stepfather Kaiza Gatō executed in public, says resisting is
  pointless.
- **Climax:** On the unfinished bridge Haku fights Naruto and Sasuke and seems to kill Sasuke.
  Haku throws himself in front of Kakashi's Lightning Cutter to save Zabuza and dies. Gatō arrives
  with hired thugs, meaning to kill Zabuza so he won't have to pay him, and kicks Haku's body.
  Naruto shames Zabuza; Zabuza kills Gatō with a kunai held in his teeth and dies beside Haku.
  Inari brings the villagers to the bridge, and Gatō's men run.
- **Moral grey:** The antagonists are sympathetic: Haku is a tool by choice, devoted to the man who
  took him in, and Zabuza grieves him in the end. The real villain is the employer, who betrays his
  own hired blades.
- **Leaves behind:** The bridge is named the Great Naruto Bridge. Naruto's way of the ninja (never
  go back on his word) is set. Zabuza's sword and Haku's story return in Part II.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_%E2%80%94_Land_of_Waves,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A_Dangerous_Mission!_Journey_to_the_Land_of_Waves!,

  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gat%C5%8D, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Haku,

### Chūnin Exams

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 34–115, episodes 20–67. Canon. An exam, not a mission; since the Third
  Shinobi World War it has been held twice a year, open to allied villages, hosts taking turns.
- **Premise:** The jōnin senseis put their teams forward. Genin must enter as three-man teams.
- **First exam (written test):** The proctor Ibiki Morino gives a paper no genin can answer; the
  first nine questions are meant to be answered by cheating without being caught (a test of
  information-gathering). The tenth question comes with a threat: skip it and your whole team
  fails; answer wrong and you stay a genin forever. Many quit. Naruto refuses to, and steadies the
  room. Everyone who chose to take the question passes: choosing to stay is the answer.
- **Second exam (Forest of Death):** Each team gets a Heaven or an Earth scroll and must get the
  other (by force or trade), then reach the tower in the middle within five days as a whole team.
  Anko finds the bodies of three Grass genin; one was Shiore, whose face was stolen. "Shiore" is
  Orochimaru, who corners Team 7, puts the Cursed Seal of Heaven on Sasuke and sends his Sound
  genin after him. Kabuto Yakushi, a friendly Leaf genin who helps the team, turns out at the end
  of the arc to be working for Orochimaru.
- **Preliminaries:** Seven teams pass, more than twice as many as expected, so one-on-one bouts thin
  them. Neji nearly kills his cousin Hinata (main versus branch house of the Hyūga). Gaara crushes
  Rock Lee's arm and leg.
- **Third exam (finals):** A month to train. Naruto beats Neji, breaking his belief in fate.
  Shikamaru outthinks Temari, then forfeits. Sasuke arrives late, his match held back at the
  "Kazekage's" request.
- **Leaves behind:** Promotion is judged on qualities shown, not wins: the Third confirms this, and
  Shikamaru, though he lost, is the only one promoted to chūnin that exam. Orochimaru's interest in
  Sasuke, the cursed seal, Neji's change and Gaara's grudge all carry on. The exam itself is the
  cover for the next arc.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_(Arc),
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Idate_Morino

### Konoha Crush

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 116–138, episodes 68–80. Canon.
- **Premise:** The exam finals were the cover for an invasion by Sound and Sand, an ally of the
  Leaf. The Fourth Kazekage agreed to Orochimaru's plan to win back funding his daimyō had cut;
  Orochimaru then murdered him and took his place on the day of the finals.
- **Beats:** At a signal during the finals (Kabuto's sleep genjutsu over the crowd), Sand and Sound
  shinobi attack, and giant snakes break the walls. The "Fourth Kazekage" beside the Third Hokage
  takes him hostage; it is Orochimaru. His Sound bodyguards raise a barrier around the two.
  Orochimaru brings back the First and Second Hokage; the Third uses the Dead Demon Consuming Seal,
  taking the dead Hokage's souls and then Orochimaru's arms, and dies.
- **Parallel thread:** Gaara, the Sand's jinchūriki (the One-Tails), is hurt and pulled out of the
  fight. Sasuke chases him, with Naruto, Sakura and Shikamaru sent after him (the wiki's mission
  list calls this an A-rank). Gaara turns into the Shukaku. Naruto summons Gamabunta and wins with
  a headbutt. Gaara realises Naruto's strength comes from protecting people.
- **The turn:** The Sand was a willing partner, then a pawn: its Kage had agreed to the invasion,
  but Orochimaru killed him and wore his face to keep the Sand in line. The invasion falls apart
  when the Third dies and Gaara loses.
- **Leaves behind:** The Hokage is dead (a funeral, and the search for a successor). Orochimaru
  can't use his arms and needs a new body (Sasuke). The Sand finds its Kage's body, surrenders,
  apologises and signs a peace treaty with the Leaf. Gaara begins to change.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_(Arc),
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_(Kakashi),
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Search_for_Tsunade

### Search for Tsunade

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 139–171, episodes 81–100. Canon. B-rank as a mission (per a databook
  the wiki cites).
- **Premise:** The village elders ask Jiraiya to be Hokage. He declines and names Tsunade, whose
  whereabouts are unknown. He sets off to find her, on condition he can take Naruto.
- **Beats:** Itachi and Kisame (Akatsuki, first time on screen) slip into the Leaf, beat Asuma and
  Kurenai and put Kakashi down with Itachi's Mangekyō Sharingan. They are after the Nine-Tails in
  Naruto, and Jiraiya stops them. Sasuke, chasing his brother, is beaten again. On the road Naruto
  learns the Rasengan. They find Tsunade, an unlucky gambler, in Tanzaku Town (the wiki's
  "Tanzaku Quarters", a gambling town).
- **The other offer:** Orochimaru reached Tsunade first. He wants his arms healed and offers to bring
  back her brother Nawaki and her lover Dan. She is tempted, and he gives her a week.
- **The wager:** Tsunade bets Naruto can't master the Rasengan in a week: the First Hokage's necklace
  against all the money in his wallet. The necklace is "cursed"; the two she gave it to both died
  bloody deaths, and since then she is afraid of blood.
- **Climax:** Tsunade nearly heals Orochimaru, then attacks him instead. Kabuto takes Naruto on, and
  Naruto lands a Rasengan. Tsunade overcomes her fear of blood to shield Naruto. The three Sannin
  summon and fight; Orochimaru withdraws.
- **Leaves behind:** Tsunade gives Naruto the necklace and becomes the Fifth Hokage, then heals
  Sasuke and Kakashi and later operates on Lee. Akatsuki's hunt for jinchūriki is in the open.
  Sasuke's grudge deepens.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Search_for_Tsunade,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tanzaku_Town

### Sasuke Recovery Mission

- **Where:** Part I, chapters 172–238, episodes 107–135. Canon. A-rank (the Sasuke Recovery Team
  page, citing a databook).
- **Premise:** Sasuke leaves the village to go to Orochimaru, escorted by the Sound Four. With most
  jōnin out on missions to keep the village's workload up, Tsunade can't staff an A-rank job
  properly, so she gives it to the newly made chūnin Shikamaru and whatever genin he can gather in
  half an hour.
- **Setup beats:** Sasuke's rooftop fight with Naruto, broken up by Kakashi, and the Sound Four's
  offer of power push him over the line. Sakura begs him to stay; he knocks her out and leaves.
  She then begs Naruto to bring him home, and he makes her "a promise of a lifetime".
- **Structure:** A relay. Shikamaru recruits Naruto, Chōji, Kiba (with Akamaru) and Neji (Shino is
  away; Lee, still recovering from surgery, recommends Neji). At each Sound Four member, one member
  stays behind to fight so the rest can go on: Chōji against Jirōbō, Neji against Kidōmaru, Kiba
  against Sakon and Ukon, Shikamaru against Tayuya. Then Kimimaro comes to meet them; Naruto
  fights him until Sasuke breaks out of his barrel and runs, and Lee, arriving late, holds Kimimaro
  so Naruto can follow. Kiba and Shikamaru are about to die when the Sand siblings, sent as
  reinforcements, arrive; Gaara saves Lee, and Kimimaro dies of his illness mid-fight.
- **Climax:** Naruto catches Sasuke at the Valley of the End. Sasuke's cursed seal, boosted by a
  pill from the Sound Four, reaches level two; Naruto draws on the Nine-Tails' cloak. Sasuke's
  Sharingan matures, he wins, spares Naruto and goes on.
- **Ending:** The mission fails. Neji and Chōji nearly die, Kiba is hurt, Shikamaru only breaks a
  finger and wants to quit until his father talks him round. Sakura overhears that Naruto failed;
  he tells her he will still keep his promise, and she thanks him. Sakura becomes Tsunade's
  apprentice, and Naruto leaves to train with Jiraiya for two and a half years.
- **Leaves behind:** The unkept promise drives all of Part II. The Sand, enemies a few months
  earlier, are allies now.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Team, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Missions

### Kakashi Gaiden (Kannabi Bridge)

- **Where:** Canon side story, chapters 239–244, set during the Third Shinobi World War, published
  between Part I and Part II. Animated in Shippūden episodes 119–120.
- **Premise:** Kakashi has just been made jōnin. Minato sends his team (Kakashi, Obito, Rin) to
  destroy a bridge (the Kannabi Bridge) that supplies Iwagakure's forces, through Grass country,
  then splits off to fight the main force, leaving Kakashi in command.
- **Beats:** Kakashi puts the mission above comrades, by the rules his father was shamed for
  breaking. Rin is taken by Iwa ninja. Obito goes after her alone; Kakashi walks the other way,
  then turns back. Kakashi loses his left eye saving Obito, and Obito awakens his Sharingan. They
  free Rin, a cave-in crushes Obito, and he gives Kakashi his left Sharingan as the promotion gift
  he had forgotten to bring. Kakashi kills the last captor, faints facing more Iwa ninja, Minato
  arrives, and the bridge is destroyed.
- **Leaves behind:** Kakashi's creed (those who abandon comrades are worse than scum), his borrowed
  eye and the name "Copy Ninja". Obito's "death" sets up the series' biggest reveal.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Gaiden

## Part I filler (anime only)

### Land of Tea Escort Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 102–106. Filler. B-rank on the wiki's mission list (Tsunade says it
  may be A-rank), given to genin because the jōnin are short after the invasion.
- **Premise:** Team 7 (without Kakashi) is to escort a runner for the Wasabi family in the Land of
  Tea's dangerous annual race, because the rival Wagarashi family is said to have hired ninja to
  sabotage it.
- **The charge:** On the road they meet an arrogant youth who hates ninja and sticks them with his
  tea-house bill. At the Wasabi house he turns out to be their runner, Idate. Mid-race it comes out
  that he is Ibiki's younger brother and a former Leaf genin who fled the village.
- **Beats:** The antagonist, Aoi Rokushō, a Leaf shinobi who defected to Amegakure, was Idate's
  teacher: after Idate failed Ibiki's chūnin exam, Aoi tricked him into stealing a secret scroll
  and the Sword of the Thunder God, so Idate ran. Aoi now fights with that sword. Sasuke weakens it
  with Chidori and Naruto breaks it and beats Aoi with a Rasengan.
- **Ending:** Idate wins the race. The Wagarashi head tries to cheat the result, the daimyō catches
  him and his minister, the minister has to become a monk and the Wagarashi family is disbanded.
  Idate and Ibiki make peace; Idate stays in the Land of Tea with the Wasabi family.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Tea_Escort_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Idate_Morino, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Aoi_Rokush%C5%8D,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_(Kakashi)

### Land of Rice Fields Investigation Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 136–141. Filler (English dub: Land of Rice Paddies, later the Land of
  Sound).
- **Premise:** Naruto and Sakura set out on their own to save Sasuke. Jiraiya catches them at the
  border and takes them along on his own mission, gathering information on Orochimaru and Sasuke
  in the Land of Rice Fields (home of the Sound village), so they aren't branded missing-nin.
- **Beats:** They meet Sasame of the Fūma clan, looking for her cousin Arashi, who went missing with
  most of the clan. Sasame is not what she seems: she staged a fight with three Fūma ninja to win
  their trust and drugged their tea to hand them over in exchange for seeing Arashi, until the
  Fūma revealed they meant to kill them. Jiraiya learns from Fūma who refused Orochimaru what he
  is after. In the hideout the team splits up. A "Kabuto" turns out to be the Fūma kunoichi Kagerō
  in disguise; she dies after warning Sasame how much Arashi has changed.
- **The turn:** The Fūma joined Orochimaru for a promise of restoring their clan. Arashi was fused
  with two other clan members by an unstable technique and driven mad.
- **Ending:** Naruto, Sasame and Sakura bring Arashi back to himself. Sasuke is not found (Arashi
  says only that he is safe for now), but the clan is freed from Orochimaru. Hanzaki, the Fūma
  leader who refused the bargain, takes in Sasame.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Rice_Fields_Investigation_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasame, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kager%C5%8D,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hanzaki, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Rice_Fields

### Mizuki Tracking Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 142–147. Filler.
- **Premise:** Mizuki, the chūnin who tricked Naruto into stealing the Scroll of Seals in the first
  arc, breaks out of the Leaf's prison along with two other inmates (the Fūjin-Raijin "Legendary
  Stupid Brothers"). Naruto, spying on a meeting, learns he has a link to Orochimaru and goes after
  him with Iruka.
- **Beats:** Team 10 takes over the fight with the brothers. Mizuki's former lover Tsubaki helps
  Naruto and Iruka find his hideout, where he has Orochimaru's instructions for a strength-giving
  potion. He drinks it and turns into a tiger-like brute.
- **Twist:** The formula is an unfinished prototype cursed seal (the anime's Animal Cursed Seal):
  the more chakra he pours into strength, the slower he gets, and it then ages him into a frail old
  man. It was a failed experiment of Orochimaru's.
- **Ending:** Naruto and Iruka beat him with a Rasengan. Tsunade saves his life, but he loses his
  ninja abilities; questioned, he knows nothing of Orochimaru's whereabouts and goes back to
  prison. The arc closes the series' first villain and the first Naruto-Iruka bond.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki_Tracking_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Legendary_Stupid_Brothers

### Bikōchū Search Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 148–151. Filler.
- **Premise:** Team 8 asks Tsunade for a mission to find the bikōchū, a rare beetle that tracks any
  scent it has learned, so it can track Sasuke. Kurenai is still in hospital, so Shino leads.
  Naruto, caught spying, is added on one condition: if this fails, he stops asking for missions to
  find Sasuke.
- **Beats:** The Kamizuru clan of Iwagakure, bee users, also want the beetle to restore their clan's
  honour. The team catches a bikōchū and it lays an egg. Hinata is taken hostage for the egg, the
  team is trapped trying to free her, she escapes and saves them with a new technique of her own
  (which Naruto inspired).
- **Ending:** The mission fails by accident: Naruto can't find Sasuke's forehead protector for the
  scent and breaks wind as the egg hatches, so the hatchling learns his scent instead, and a
  bikōchū learns only one scent in its life. The next egg is a year away, and it was the last
  chance Tsunade had allowed.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bik%C5%8Dch%C5%AB_Search_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bik%C5%8Dch%C5%AB

### Kurosuki Family Removal Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 152–157. Filler.
- **Premise:** Three injured men from the Katabami Gold Mine village ask the Leaf for help against
  the gang terrorising them. Tsunade sends Naruto with Team Guy, partly to keep him from obsessing
  over Sasuke and Jiraiya. Naruto hopes the gang's leader, a former Mist Swordsman like Kisame,
  will know something.
- **Beats:** The villagers are so afraid that they refuse to be rescued. The leader, Raiga Kurosuki,
  has a mad love of funerals and buries people alive. Much of his power, including a genjutsu even
  the Byakugan can't see through, comes from Ranmaru, a boy with a rare dōjutsu who rides on his
  back.
- **Moral grey:** Ranmaru is not a villain: he has stayed with Raiga for years by choice, because
  they understood each other and Raiga gave him a reason to live. When Raiga is dying, Ranmaru gives
  almost all his own strength to save him.
- **Ending:** Once Ranmaru chooses another life and Naruto overpowers him, Raiga gives himself his
  own "funeral" with his lightning. Ranmaru stays behind as an apprentice to Sanshō, the old cook
  of the Curry of Life shop, and the team goes home.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurosuki_Family_Removal_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ranmaru

### Star Guard Mission (Hoshigakure)

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 178–183. Filler.
- **Premise:** Team Guy is sent to help Hoshigakure guard its "star", a meteorite, from thieves;
  Naruto talks his way on because he suspects Orochimaru wants it.
- **Beats:** Sumaru, a young genin of the village, guides them past its poison gases. The star's
  chakra makes a special training possible, and that training kills most who try it. The Third
  Hoshikage stopped it; the acting Hoshikage, Akahoshi, started it again. A masked thief steals
  the star.
- **Twist:** The thief is Natsuhi, Sumaru's mother, who finished the training and faked her death
  years ago when the Third agreed to stop it. Akahoshi killed the Third Hoshikage to take over;
  he takes Sumaru hostage to force her to give up the star and later kills her.
- **The bind:** Neji warns Naruto that Akahoshi is the village's own leader, so the Leaf can't act
  against him without consequences. Only when a Hoshi genin shows the villagers what the training
  did to his body, and the truth about the Third comes out, do the villagers turn on him.
- **Ending:** Akahoshi fuses the star into his body; Naruto destroys it with a Rasengan. The
  afflicted Hoshi ninja are treated in the Leaf. The village loses its source of power and keeps
  its children.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Guard_Mission

### Yakumo Kurama Rescue Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 203–207. Filler.
- **Premise:** Naruto watches a girl paint lightning striking the village; lightning strikes the
  Hokage's residence. Kurenai has given up leading Team 8 to deal with her old student, Yakumo
  Kurama, whose genjutsu is so strong it can do real harm, even kill. Naruto then joins Guy, Sakura
  and Team 8 on a mission to protect Yakumo from her own clan.
- **Beats:** Yakumo's uncle Unkai leads clan members against her because they fear her power.
  Returning, the team finds the Leaf in ruins; it is a vast genjutsu of Yakumo's. Without proper
  training her bloodline grew a second, murderous personality, Ido. Unkai sacrifices himself to
  free Naruto from the illusion.
- **The grey:** On the Third Hokage's order, Kurenai refused to train Yakumo and sealed her power
  before Ido could take over, so the "rescuer" is also the one who crippled her. Ido broke the seal
  and cast the fire that killed her parents, but Yakumo believes the Third ordered it.
- **Ending:** Naruto tells Yakumo the truth about her power, which brings back the memory of her
  parents' death and frees Ido. Kurenai helps her face it, and she defeats it. Kurenai, her last
  mission from the Third done, goes back to Team 8.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yakumo_Kurama_Rescue_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yakumo_Kurama

### Gantetsu Escort Mission

- **Where:** Part I, episodes 209–212. Filler.
- **Premise:** Naruto, Sakura and Lee help transfer a prisoner, Gantetsu of the criminal Shinobazu
  gang, to the Land of Forests' capital for questioning and trial, and must guard him from the
  gang led by Shura.
- **Beats:** The head guard, Todoroki, hates Gantetsu: the Shinobazu, Gantetsu among them, killed
  his family five years ago. Lost in the Shinobazu's maze-like forest, Gantetsu guides the guards
  and says the gang wants back money he stole from them.
- **Twist (the charge isn't who he seems):** Gantetsu turned on the gang years ago and has been
  saving children from its raids and raising them, starting with Akio, Todoroki's younger brother.
  He stole the loot and let himself be caught on purpose, to draw the Shinobazu out where they
  could be stopped for good.
- **Ending:** Naruto beats Shura and the Shinobazu are destroyed. Todoroki still won't forgive him,
  but lets Gantetsu go to watch over the orphans, reporting him dead in the fire, and lets Akio stay
  with him.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gantetsu_Escort_Mission

## Part II canon (Shippūden)

### Kazekage Rescue Mission

- **Where:** Part II, chapters 245–281 (volumes 28–32), Shippūden episodes 1–32. Canon. A-rank
  (S-rank in the anime).
- **Premise:** Gaara, now Kazekage, is taken by Akatsuki's Deidara and Sasori for the One-Tails.
  Team Kakashi (Naruto, Sakura, Kakashi) is sent to the Sand to help; Team Guy follows.
- **Beats:** Sasori has a sleeper agent in the Sand, Yūra, a trusted member of its council.
  Kankurō goes after the pair and is poisoned by Sasori. Sakura draws out the poison and makes
  antidotes, impressing the old puppeteer Chiyo, Sasori's grandmother, who joins the rescue.
- **Delays:** Akatsuki's leader sends copies of Itachi and Kisame (other people, transformed) to
  hold off both teams, and a failsafe at the lair makes clones of Team Guy. The fights only buy
  time for the three-day extraction.
- **Climax:** At the lair, Gaara is already dead. Sakura and Chiyo fight Sasori; Sakura's antidotes
  and Chiyo's puppets win, they kill him, and Chiyo uses her life-giving technique to save the
  dying Sakura. Sasori, dying, tells Sakura of a meeting with his
  spy in Orochimaru's camp in ten days. Naruto and Kakashi chase Deidara, who seems to blow himself
  up but escapes.
- **Ending:** Naruto blames Chiyo for making Gaara a jinchūriki. Chiyo gives her life to revive
  him, with Naruto lending her chakra, and Sand shinobi run out to greet him: the boy the village
  feared now has its love. Chiyo's funeral.
- **Leaves behind:** Sasori's spy meeting starts the next arc. Chiyo's change of heart about
  jinchūriki. Deidara survives, and Tobi joins Akatsuki in Sasori's place.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage_Rescue_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Y%C5%ABra, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiyo,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_(Kakashi)

### Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission

- **Where:** Part II, chapters 282–310, Shippūden episodes 33–53. Canon. Anime title
  "Long-Awaited Reunion". A-rank; recorded as a failure.
- **Premise:** With Kakashi in hospital, Team Kakashi is rebuilt with Sai, a member of Danzō's
  Root sent to replace Sasuke, and Tenzō of the Anbu as captain under the codename Yamato (the
  elders only let Naruto go with him in charge). The job: go to the Tenchi Bridge in Grass country
  in place of the dead Sasori and meet his spy in Orochimaru's camp.
- **Beats:** Yamato goes disguised as Sasori. The spy is Kabuto, but Sasori's hold on him was broken
  long ago, and Orochimaru comes with him; the meeting turns into a fight. Naruto loses control in
  his four-tailed state and hurts Sakura.
- **Twist (double agent):** Sai goes over to Orochimaru as Danzō's messenger, offering an alliance
  for Danzō's plot against the Leaf. Yamato tracks him by a seed hidden in his food. Caught, Sai
  says he is spying on Orochimaru for Danzō; then a Bingo Book in his bag shows the other part of
  his orders: kill Sasuke.
- **Climax:** Inside the hideout, Sai, shaken by Naruto's loyalty to Sasuke, chooses the bond over
  his orders and sides with the team. They find Sasuke, who brushes them aside.
- **Ending:** Sasuke leaves with Orochimaru and Kabuto. Sai stays with the team, learning about
  bonds.
- **Leaves behind:** Danzō is clearly playing his own game. Sai becomes a real teammate.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenchi_Bridge_Reconnaissance_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_(Kakashi)

### Akatsuki Suppression Mission (Hidan and Kakuzu)

- **Where:** Part II, chapters 311–342, Shippūden episodes 72–88. Canon.
- **Premise:** Hidan and Kakuzu kill Chiriku, head monk of the Fire Temple and Asuma's fellow
  former Guardian Ninja, and cash in his body for the bounty. Tsunade sends out a new task force:
  Asuma, Shikamaru, Izumo and Kotetsu.
- **Beats:** Hidan's curse links his body to anyone whose blood he has tasted. Shikamaru works out
  that it holds only while Hidan stands in his ritual circle, and forces him out; Asuma cuts off his
  head, but Kakuzu sews it back on, Hidan gets back into his circle and kills Asuma by stabbing
  himself.
- **Asuma's death:** Ino and Chōji arrive with backup and the Akatsuki pair are called away. Asuma
  dies after a last cigarette and a word for each student. Shikamaru later understands the "king"
  of Asuma's shōgi lesson as the next generation (Kurenai is carrying Asuma's child).
- **Revenge:** Tsunade won't let Team 10 go without a captain, so Kakashi volunteers to lead them.
  Shikamaru separates Hidan from Kakuzu, drags him to ground he rigged in advance in his clan's
  forest, and tricks him: the blood Hidan used for his curse was Kakuzu's. Then he buries Hidan,
  still alive, in a pit of explosive tags. Naruto arrives with his new Wind Release: Rasenshuriken,
  which destroys two of Kakuzu's hearts and leaves him unable to move; Kakashi finishes him.
  Tsunade later forbids the technique because it damages Naruto's own arm.
- **Leaves behind:** Team 10 has lost its sensei. Shikamaru's grief turns into him becoming a
  leader. Hidan is not dead, only buried.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki_Suppression_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Rasenshuriken,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ry%C5%8D

### Itachi Pursuit Mission (and Fated Battle Between Brothers)

- **Where:** Part II. The wiki splits this story: the Itachi Pursuit Mission is chapters 343–367,
  Shippūden episodes 113–118 and 121–126; the fight and the reveal are the Fated Battle Between
  Brothers, chapters 384–412, episodes 134–143, with the Tale of Jiraiya the Gallant (chapters
  368–383) between them. Canon. The mission's rank is unknown; it is recorded as a failure.
- **Premise:** Sasuke has killed (absorbed) Orochimaru and formed Hebi with three freed prisoners
  (Suigetsu, Karin, Jūgo) to hunt Itachi. The Leaf sends an eight-man squad (Team Kakashi and Team 8) to find him through Itachi.
- **The rule of the hunt:** Kakashi reasons that Itachi must be captured, not killed, since he is
  the way to Sasuke, which is why he brings in Team 8's trackers.
- **Beats:** Sasuke fights Deidara, who blows himself up; the squad heads for the blast and Kiba
  picks up Sasuke's scent, until Karin has the scent scattered. Itachi stalls Naruto with a clone,
  and Tobi stalls the squad. Sasuke and Itachi fight at the Uchiha hideout; Itachi dies poking
  Sasuke's forehead and smiling.
- **The reveal:** Tobi, calling himself Madara Uchiha, tells Sasuke that the village elders ordered
  Itachi to wipe out his clan to stop a coup. Itachi, terminally ill, meant to die by Sasuke's hand
  so Sasuke would be hailed as a hero.
- **Ending:** The Leaf squad arrives too late; Tobi takes Sasuke. Sasuke renames his team Taka and
  makes "the complete and utter destruction of Konoha" his goal, working with Akatsuki.
- **Moral grey:** The villain was the hero, the village the culprit. The revenge the hero lived for
  turns into revenge on the hero's own side.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Pursuit_Mission,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fated_Battle_Between_Brothers,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tale_of_Jiraiya_the_Gallant,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_(Kakashi)

### Pain's Assault

- **Where:** Part II, chapters 413–453, Shippūden episodes 152–169 and 172–175. Canon.
- **Premise:** Jiraiya has died spying on Akatsuki's leader, Pain, in Amegakure (the Tale of
  Jiraiya the Gallant arc), leaving a coded message. Pain attacks the Leaf to take the Nine-Tails
  while Naruto is away learning sage mode on Mount Myōboku.
- **Beats:** Pain's six bodies split up and search the village; Danzō kills the toad sent to
  summon Naruto back. No Leaf shinobi will give away where Naruto is. Kakashi dies against Pain.
  Pain flattens the village with one Shinra Tensei.
- **Climax:** Naruto returns in sage mode and takes the bodies apart. Hinata steps in, tells him she
  loves him and is struck down; Naruto loses control towards the Nine-Tails and is pulled back by
  his father Minato, sealed inside him.
- **Resolution by talk:** Naruto finds the real Pain, Nagato, a war orphan who studied under
  Jiraiya with Konan and Yahiko, and who seeks peace through fear. Naruto refuses to kill him,
  holding to what Jiraiya believed. Nagato uses his last strength to bring back everyone he killed,
  and dies.
- **Leaves behind:** Naruto is the village's hero; those who once shunned him cheer. Konan leaves
  the Akatsuki. Tsunade, drained protecting the villagers, is in a coma, and Danzō persuades the
  Fire Daimyō to make him Hokage, which sets up the next arc.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain's_Assault_(Arc)

### Five Kage Summit

- **Where:** Part II, chapters 454–483, Shippūden episodes 197–214. Canon.
- **Premise:** The Kage meet in the neutral Land of Iron to decide how to deal with Akatsuki.
  Danzō attends as the Leaf's new Hokage.
- **Beats:** Tobi sends Sasuke and Taka to the summit to kill Danzō, the man behind the Uchiha
  massacre. Ao of the Mist sees Danzō using Shisui's eye to sway the Land of Iron's leader into
  making him head of the alliance. Sasuke attacks; the Kage fight him; Danzō flees. Sakura gives
  Naruto a false confession of love to make him give up Sasuke, and goes to kill Sasuke herself.
- **Climax:** In the hall, Tobi demands Naruto and Killer B. The Kage refuse, and he declares the
  Fourth Shinobi World War. Outside, Sasuke corners Danzō, who takes Karin hostage; Sasuke stabs
  through her to kill him. Sakura reaches Sasuke, pretends to defect, and can't go through with
  killing him; Kakashi steps in.
- **Ending:** The Five Kage and the Land of Iron form the Allied Shinobi Forces, and Kakashi is put
  forward as Hokage. In the opening of the next arc (Countdown), Naruto clashes with Sasuke, and
  they part knowing their next fight may kill them both.
- **Leaves behind:** Five villages that fought each other for a century unite. Danzō's death reopens
  the Hokage question.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit_(Arc),
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Countdown

## Part II filler (anime only)

### Twelve Guardian Ninja

- **Where:** Shippūden episodes 54–71. Filler.
- **Premise:** Naruto trains wind chakra with Asuma. Team Kakashi (Naruto, Sakura, Sai, Yamato) is
  sent to the Fire Temple to protect four hidden tombs: tomb robbers are stealing the bodies of the
  Twelve Guardian Ninja (former bodyguards of the Fire Daimyō). Naruto meets Sora, a hot-headed
  boy from the temple, despised by the monks. B-rank; recorded as a failure.
- **Beats:** Sora and Naruto clash and slowly become friends. The robbers' leader, "Furido", tells
  Sora that Asuma killed his father, pushing him to kill Asuma and the Hokage, then attacks the Leaf
  with the reanimated Guardian Ninja. Sora carries Nine-Tails chakra: his father collected chakra
  that leaked during the Nine-Tails' attack on the Leaf and sealed it into him, telling no one.
- **Moral grey:** Furido is that father, Kazuma, once a Guardian Ninja himself, who tried to
  assassinate the Fire Daimyō in a coup and whom Asuma thought he had killed. Kazuma uses his son
  as a weapon.
- **Ending:** Asuma kills Kazuma. Sora loses control of the fox's chakra; Naruto reaches him and frees
  him. Sora leaves to travel the world.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twelve_Guardian_Ninja_(Arc),
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazuma, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sora,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_(Kakashi)

### Three-Tails' Appearance

- **Where:** Shippūden episodes 89–112. Filler (the longest anime-only arc).
- **Premise:** Orochimaru sends Kabuto and Guren (a crystal user, leading her own team) with a boy,
  Yūkimaru, whose chakra can draw out and control the untamed Three-Tails at a lake. The Leaf sends
  Kakashi with Team 8 to find Orochimaru's hideout, then Team Yamato, then more teams; Akatsuki
  (Tobi, Deidara) is also after the beast.
- **Beats:** Guren looks after Yūkimaru and comes to love him; Kabuto drugs him to make him use his
  power. Guren realises she is the one who killed Yūkimaru's mother. The Leaf's objective becomes
  sealing the beast with a four-corner barrier; Guren interrupts, and Yūkimaru's grief at her
  apparent death sends the beast berserk and breaks the seal.
- **Moral grey:** Guren is an enemy who comes to love the child she is using, and whose mother she
  killed; he forgives her. The beast is dangerous, not evil.
- **Ending:** The beast is driven off and the sealing left to Anbu, whom Akatsuki kill; Akatsuki
  takes the Three-Tails. Guren and Yūkimaru (with her teammate Gozu) leave together, turned away
  from Orochimaru.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three-Tails'_Appearance,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Guren, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Y%C5%ABkimaru

### Paradise Life on a Boat

- **Where:** Shippūden episodes 223–242, in the middle of the countdown to the war. Filler.
- **Premise:** To keep Naruto out of Akatsuki's reach during the war, he is escorted to an isolated
  island in the Land of Lightning by sea, by the long way on Tsunade's orders, in a small boat,
  with Yamato, Aoba, Guy and three other shinobi.
- **Structure:** A string of mostly unconnected stories. On the voyage: a fisherman's hunt for a
  giant marlin that killed his father, a ghost ship, a pirate-held battleship island, a monster on a
  forgotten island, a mushroom that eats chakra, a rebellion of Naruto's shadow clones, a Naruto
  impostor. Back home, side stories for the rest of the Konoha 11 (a girls' night out, Sai's day
  off, Ino-Shika-Chō, Kiba, Guy and Kakashi's rivalry). The last one ends a grudge between the Mist
  and the Rock.
- **Ending:** They wait for a ship to the island (the Island Turtle). On the island, Naruto's cover
  story is that he is "examining the island's ecology". The episodes are a road anthology between
  big arcs.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Paradise_Life_on_a_Boat,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Island_Turtle,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Countdown

### Power

- **Where:** Shippūden episodes 290–295. Filler (the "Power" arc, set just after the Five Kage
  Summit).
- **Premise:** Team Kakashi investigates the massacre of Tonika Village near a place called "the
  Hole", attacked by reanimated shinobi. They are helped by the neighbouring Hachō Village and its
  leader, Disonasu.
- **Beats:** Kabuto is behind the attack, using the Hole's spring water and tiny snakes to clone
  Hidan from his DNA, and later a clone of Naruto in his Nine-Tails form. The village's teacher,
  Dokku, cares for the surviving orphans.
- **Twist:** Disonasu, the helpful neighbour, hired Kabuto to wipe out Tonika so he could raise the
  buried "power" (the Ama no Hoko); he is a former Akatsuki subordinate.
- **Ending:** Naruto destroys his clone in a new form, the power is sealed back in the Hole, and
  Kabuto slips away. Dokku and Hachō's officer Shiseru marry and adopt the orphans.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Power_(Arc),
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Power_-_Episode_1

### Kakashi's Anbu Arc: The Shinobi That Lives in the Darkness

- **Where:** Shippūden episodes 349–361. Filler (flashback). English TV title "Kakashi: Shadow of
  the Anbu Black Ops".
- **Premise:** Young Kakashi, still guilty over Rin's death, is put in the Anbu by Minato. After the
  Nine-Tails' attack, Danzō's Root has a boy, Kinoe (later Tenzō, then Yamato), who can use Wood
  Release, the product of the village's early experiments.
- **Beats:** Kakashi stops a Root plot on the Third Hokage and lets Kinoe, the would-be assassin,
  escape. Hunting the defecting Orochimaru, the two meet the Iburi clan, smoke users loyal to
  Orochimaru; he slaughters them, and Kinoe's Wood Release holds together the last of them, Yukimi,
  whose body is turning to smoke. Later Danzō orders Kinoe to take Kakashi's Sharingan; he can't,
  Danzō moves to seal him, and the Third gets him out of Root into the Anbu. Itachi joins the Anbu,
  watched by both the clan and Danzō, up to the Uchiha massacre.
- **Ending:** Kakashi keeps the Iburi clan and Orochimaru out of his report, protecting the people
  involved. After the massacre he is taken out of the Anbu and made a jōnin sensei; he fails every
  team until Naruto, Sasuke and Sakura break his rule for each other.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi's_Anbu_Arc:_The_Shinobi_That_Lives_in_the_Darkness

## Movies

### Ninja Clash in the Land of Snow (2004)

- **Premise:** Team 7 is assigned to guard the actress Yukie Fujikaze while a film is shot in the
  Land of Snow. (Kakashi first sends them to watch her film as preparation.)
- **Twist:** Yukie is Koyuki Kazahana, the princess of the Land of Snow, whom Kakashi saved as a

  Spring. Sandayū dies leading a samurai charge. Naruto beats Dotō; Koyuki takes up her role as
  daimyō while still acting.

### Guardians of the Crescent Moon Kingdom (2006)

### The Lost Tower (Shippūden film, 2010)

- **Premise:** Team Yamato (Naruto, Sakura, Sai, Yamato) hunts the missing-nin Mukade in the ruins of

## Mission ranks and small missions

### The rank scale (Narutopedia "Missions" and rank pages)

- **D-rank:** For genin fresh from the Academy; almost no risk, odd jobs like farming and
  babysitting (in the story also finding pets, weeding, walking dogs). Pay 5,000–50,000 ryō.
- **C-rank:** More experienced genin or chūnin. Guarding people against bandits or highwaymen,
  background investigations, catching bandits or thieves, dealing with wild animals; little to no
  chance of fighting other ninja. Pay 30,000–100,000 ryō.
- **B-rank:** Experienced chūnin; fighting other ninja is expected (guarding people, espionage,
  killing ninja). Pay 80,000–200,000 ryō.
- **A-rank:** Jōnin; village- or state-level matters (guarding VIPs, suppressing ninja forces). Pay
  150,000–1,000,000 ryō.
- **S-rank:** Experienced jōnin; state-level secrets (assassinating VIPs, carrying classified
  documents). Pay at least 1,000,000 ryō.
- **Currency:** 1 ryō is 10 yen.
- **How it is used in the story:** The rank is set by what the client says the danger is, and the
  client pays for it. A client who downplays the danger (Tazuna) frees the ninja from the contract,
  though they may carry on by choice; that is the Land of Waves hook. Mercenaries are cheaper and
  unranked, and riskier for the client (Gatō and Zabuza). Missions are handed out at the Mission
  Assignment Desk by the Hokage with Academy chūnin (Iruka lectures Naruto that beginners start
  with simple missions). Failure costs differently by village and job: Shikamaru is comforted,
  Kakashi's father Sakumo was disgraced for saving comrades over a mission.
- **Sources:** https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Missions, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/D-rank,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ry%C5%8D, https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_Assignment_Desk,
  https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A_Dangerous_Mission!_Journey_to_the_Land_of_Waves!

### Small missions shown in the series

- **Catching Tora (D-rank, chapter 9, episode 6):** Team 7 tracks down Tora, the lost cat of the
  Fire Daimyō's wife Madam Shijimi; the cat keeps running away because she nearly squeezes the
  life out of it. In Shippūden episode 1, Team Ebisu (Konohamaru's team) catches the same cat,
  asks for a C-rank as Team 7 did, is refused, and is sent after Tora six more times: a running gag
  that passes the job to the next generation.
- **The other D-ranks offered that day:** the Third Hokage lists babysitting the chief counsellor's
  three-year-old (episode 6). Shopping for a neighbour and helping dig potatoes are also remembered
  from the episode (not confirmed in sources). Naruto refuses and demands a real job.
- **D-rank versus a C-rank compromise (Shippūden episode 7, "Run, Kankurō"):** Tsunade means to give
  the new Team Kakashi a B-rank; Shizune suggests walking someone's dog, since Naruto, as a
  jinchūriki, shouldn't leave the village; Tsunade compromises with a C-rank escort of a gold
  convoy, overtaken that same day by news of the Kazekage's capture.
- **After the Land of Waves:** the team is back to pulling weeds, picking up litter and walking
  dogs, and Kakashi notes their teamwork slipping.
- **First C-rank (Land of Waves):** a bodyguard job against bandits, which the client under-ranked.
  See above.
- **Training as missions:** Chūnin Exam stages, the bell test and the Rasengan wager all work as
  jobs with stated terms and a hidden real test (teamwork, nerve, conduct).
