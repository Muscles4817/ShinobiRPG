## What moved

- **The taijutsu specialist is a specialist now.** Might Guy against Kakashi went from 27% to
  77%. Guy no longer runs dry: his kicks cost breath, and a chain finishes what a daze opens
  (watched: Leaf Gust dazes Kakashi and Little Toe Attack follows in the same turn). Against
  Hiruzen he is where he was.
- **Fights at the top are a little longer.** Kakashi against Asuma 1.6 → 2.0 rounds, against
  Hiruzen 1.5 → 2.2, against Zabuza 1.1 → 1.6, Kurenai against Kakashi 2.2 → 3.0. That is the
  retune's technique weight at work: a big jōnin technique no longer counts as two and a half
  blows.
- **Sasuke against Iruka evened out** (83% → 57%, 3.3 → 4.7 rounds): a genin genius against an
  ordinary chūnin is a real fight rather than a formality.
- **Lee against Neji got faster, not closer** (still 100%, 1.9 → 1.2 rounds). Lee's moves cost
  breath and chain; Neji's Gentle Fist palms mould chakra (`moulded`) and still cost it.

## What did not move (the next retune)

- **Equals of a rank still mostly rout each other.** Naruto against Sasuke 0%, Kiba against Shino
  100%, Asuma against Kurenai 3% in 1.3 rounds. These are matchups, not grades: one kit answers
  the other's every turn (Asuma's Tree Binding against Kurenai is still pin, then hit the
  pinned), and nothing in the body arts touches them.
- **Jōnin fights are still short.** Most top matchups end in one to two rounds; the target for
  equals is two to three.
- **Body over mind is total** (Chōji 97%, Chōza 100%). The Nara bind doesn't hold a large body
  long enough to matter; size's toughness and the grip of a shadow pin want a look together.
- **Cross-rank stays absolute** (every genin against a chūnin or jōnin at 0% bar Sasuke), which
  is as intended: a grade of difference is decisive.
