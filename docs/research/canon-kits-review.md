# Canon kits from Narutopedia: review sheet

What `prototypes/narutopedia/` gave the `naruto` fan pack's versus fighters, to read and correct.
The kits live in `src/content/packs/naruto/canonKits/kits.ts` (drop an id to take a technique
back off someone) and the drafted techniques in `src/content/packs/naruto/techniques/canon/wiki/`
(edit them freely).

**175 techniques drafted, 319 additions across 66 fighters, 21 wiki jutsu found to be the pack's own under another name, 63 jutsu skipped.**

- 87: a bloodline power they don’t use in the pack
- 39: summoning needs a contract
- 22: tailed-beast powers are not modelled yet
- 18: sage arts belong to the sage ladder
- 9: a body ability, not a technique
- 7: no fight role on the wiki
- 5: Six Paths and Rinnegan paths are perks or not modelled
- 4: tailed or full-body transformations are not modelled
- 3: the Gates are the pack’s ladder
- 2: an eye is a perk, not a technique
